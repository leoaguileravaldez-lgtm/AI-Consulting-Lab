# Evidence, Analysis, and Judgment Boundaries

## Exclusive evidence authority

Layer 04 remains the exclusive authority for sources, claims, evidence records, provenance assessment, corroboration, evidence quality, confidence, freshness for evidentiary use, applicability, contradiction resolution for evidence, and validation status.

Layer 10 may bind exact Layer 04 references to a client instance and engagement. It cannot create, modify, validate, upgrade, downgrade, reinterpret, substitute, suppress, promote, supersede, or delete evidence. A client assertion or material needed analytically must enter Layer 04 intake independently.

CRM fields are not evidence. Client statements are not automatically verified facts. Commercial priority is not evidence. Client preference is not analytical truth. Repeated retrieval is not validation. Human Principal approval cannot convert an unsupported claim into evidence.

## Analysis and professional judgment

Layer 02 and the canonical analytical workflow own analysis, inferences, scenarios, recommendations, and professional judgments. Layer 10 only preserves typed, versioned references. A client fact is not an analytical conclusion; a conclusion is not a professional opinion; a professional opinion is not universal fact.

Layer 10 cannot select a convenient conclusion, conceal dissent, modify assumptions, soften limitations, change confidence, waive qualified review, or turn client acceptance into analytical validation.

## Challenge, risk, QA, and deliverables

Layer 05 dissent and challenge, Layer 06 risk/QA/findings/professional review, and Layer 07 deliverable content/readiness/release remain immutable canonical dependencies. Client pressure, preference, revenue, relationship, deadline, opportunity stage, or acceptance cannot suppress or change them.

Deliverable association requires exact client/entity/engagement, artifact/version, classification, recipient-purpose metadata, and Layer 07 readiness reference. Association cannot authorize release, delivery, publication, or external communication.
