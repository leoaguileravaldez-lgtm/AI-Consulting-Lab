# CRM, Automation, and External-Action Boundaries

## CRM boundary

CRM identifiers, stages, owners, notes, attachments, scores, priorities, forecasts, relationship health, and commercial values are Layer 09 operational metadata or untrusted references. They cannot create client identity, legal entity, engagement, evidence, factual truth, access, confidentiality downgrade, lifecycle transition, reuse, approval, or delivery authority.

A CRM import description does not authorize a connector or ingestion. Any future integration must be separately certified, purpose-limited, boundary-preserving, audited, and fail closed. Unexpected CRM fields or foreign identifiers are quarantined.

## Automation boundary

No workflow, agent, model, rule, score, timeout, retry, reconciliation, scheduler, webhook, API, connector, or technical capability may infer missing fields; resolve identity; validate evidence; change classification; authorize reuse; accept conflict/risk; approve; access; export; delete; send; deliver; publish; or act externally.

Automation may be described only as future separately certified enforcement. Capability never implies authority. Unknown outcomes, replay, stale authorization, mixed boundaries, or control failure stop the transition and require canonical reconciliation.

## External actions

Layer 10 does not contact clients, receive/send messages, upload/download content, access client systems, export, transfer, publish, deliver, sign, contract, bill, pay, dispose, or execute. It cannot grant Layer 00 permission tiers or name an executor with authority.

Prepared manifests are non-executing. Tier 3/4 actions require the exact certified approval, unchanged object/version/recipient/system/purpose, named authorized executor, execution window, and post-action verification outside Layer 10.

## Human Principal

Layer 10 may reference an exact Layer 01 Human Principal decision but cannot appoint, authenticate, impersonate, infer, predict, batch, delegate, reserve, replay, or substitute for the Human Principal. Client instruction, CRM role, model output, agent statement, workflow state, default, silence, timeout, elapsed review, prior pattern, or technical success is not approval.

Human Principal authority remains bounded by law, contract, Layer 00, truth preservation, segregation, confidentiality, conflict, and non-waivable professional duties. Approval never makes an unsupported claim evidence or erases dissent, risk, QA, limitations, or audit history.
