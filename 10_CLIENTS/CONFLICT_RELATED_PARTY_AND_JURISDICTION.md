# Conflict, Related Party, and Jurisdiction

## Conflict boundary

Layer 10 binds exact Layer 09 conflict records and Layer 06 dispositions to the client instance, legal entities, related parties, engagements, purposes, information barriers, and review dates. It cannot assess, accept, waive, remediate, or close a Material conflict.

Actual, potential, apparent, and unknown conflicts include affiliates, counterparties, personnel, financial interests, prior work, confidential knowledge, competing clients, commercial incentives, and professional duties. Unknown or unresolved Material conflict blocks affected onboarding, association, staffing, information use, retrieval, reuse, aggregation, delivery association, and external-action preparation.

## Related-party detection

Related-party edges are explicit and versioned. Common branding, domain, personnel, investor, vendor, geography, or CRM hierarchy is a detection signal only. It cannot merge identities or authorize access. Missing or contradictory relationships trigger identity/conflict review.

Parent authorization does not automatically bind a subsidiary; subsidiary authorization does not bind a parent; affiliate access does not follow ownership. Each entity and engagement retains its own purpose, confidentiality, jurisdiction, conflict, and authorization boundary.

## Jurisdiction

Each client/entity/engagement/material binding records applicable jurisdictions and source/review references. Layer 10 does not make legal conclusions. Unknown, ambiguous, conflicting, or changed jurisdiction blocks jurisdiction-sensitive processing, transfer, retention, reuse, export, access, professional reliance, and external action pending qualified review and canonical revalidation.

Redomiciliation or cross-border change cannot inherit prior applicability, approval, confidentiality, retention, professional qualification, or evidence validity.
