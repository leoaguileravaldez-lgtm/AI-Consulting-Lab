# Reuse, Knowledge Extraction, and Aggregation

## Default

Every client-specific fact, assertion, material, evidence reference, analysis reference, recommendation, deliverable, prompt, context, embedding, cache, summary, export, and derivative is `CLIENT_BOUND_NON_REUSABLE` by default. Missing reuse metadata means not reusable.

Layer 10 may prepare a Knowledge Extraction Request referencing exact origin objects/versions. It cannot de-identify authoritatively, generalize, aggregate, establish applicability, promote knowledge, authorize reuse, or inject knowledge into another client.

## Required gates

Any reuse requires separately evidenced:

1. lawful and contractual permission for exact origin, destination, and purpose;
2. confidentiality, ownership, jurisdiction, retention, and conflict clearance;
3. Layer 08 de-identification and re-identification-risk review;
4. Layer 08 generalization review;
5. Layer 08 applicability and reuse eligibility decision;
6. exact Human Principal authorization under certified policy;
7. destination client/engagement boundary validation;
8. Layer 04 validation for any factual or analytical use;
9. complete transformation, destination, expiry, revocation, and audit lineage.

Each gate is independent. De-identification alone never authorizes reuse. Name removal, aggregation, minimum-count threshold, similarity, public discussion, client closure, prior use, common ownership, or same client cannot substitute.

## Aggregation

Cross-client aggregation is prohibited by default. An aggregation candidate is treated as client-bound content from every contributing client. It requires explicit authorization for all contributors, anti-reidentification review for combinations and outliers, denominator/method lineage, confidentiality and conflict clearance, Layer 08 governance, and destination controls.

Small cells, rare combinations, chronology, geography, sector, counterparties, values, personnel, operating structure, or metadata that enable inference cause rejection. Aggregated output cannot become evidence or a benchmark without Layer 04 and Layer 08 processes.

## Revocation

Correction, reclassification, ownership change, consent withdrawal, contract change, jurisdiction change, conflict, re-identification risk, expiration, source invalidation, or upstream revocation blocks future use and triggers impact review. Layer 10 routes notices but cannot determine analytical invalidation or erase prior audit history.
