# Client-Bound Knowledge and Reuse

## Default non-reusability

All client-operation records, contacts, interactions, communications, preferences, opportunity data, commercial metadata, client facts, engagement associations, conflict details, and derived summaries are `CLIENT_BOUND_NON_REUSABLE` by default. The absence of a restriction never means reusable.

Layer 09 cannot promote knowledge, de-identify authoritatively, generalize, establish applicability, or authorize reuse. It may create a request referencing the exact origin object/version, client, engagement, purpose, classification, proposed transformation, and proposed destination.

## Required independent gates

Cross-client reuse requires all of the following, each separately evidenced:

1. lawful and contractual permission for the exact purpose;
2. confidentiality and conflict clearance;
3. Layer 08 de-identification and re-identification-risk review;
4. Layer 08 generalization and applicability review;
5. Layer 08 reuse eligibility and promotion decision where applicable;
6. exact Human Principal authorization within certified policy;
7. destination engagement intake and Layer 04 validation for any factual or analytical use;
8. complete origin, transformation, authorization, destination, expiry, and audit lineage.

Passing one gate cannot imply another. Name removal, aggregation, similarity, public discussion, repeated experience, expired confidentiality, common ownership, same client, or prior authorization for a different purpose is insufficient.

## Engagement-to-engagement boundary

Even within one client, one engagement's operational state and knowledge do not enter another engagement by default. Exact-purpose authorization, compatible classification, conflict review, freshness review, canonical association, and destination validation are required. Evidence and conclusions remain governed by Layer 04 and their originating certified layers.

## Revocation and invalidation

Authorization is exact-object, exact-version, origin, destination, purpose, time, and condition bound. Origin correction, classification increase, identity/entity change, contract change, consent withdrawal, jurisdiction change, conflict, re-identification risk, expiration, or upstream invalidation blocks future use and triggers traceable impact review. Layer 09 records the notice but cannot determine analytical invalidation.

Unauthorized or uncertain reuse is quarantined, not retroactively legitimized. Known destinations are notified through the canonical governance process; no external notification capability is created here.
