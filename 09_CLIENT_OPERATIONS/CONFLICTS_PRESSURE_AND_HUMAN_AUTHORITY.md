# Conflicts, Pressure, and Human Authority

## Conflict representation

Conflict records cover actual, potential, apparent, and unknown conflicts involving clients, affiliates, counterparties, personnel, interests, prior work, confidential knowledge, commercial incentives, independence, and professional duties.

Each record includes parties and canonical identifiers where known, conflict type, affected scopes, discovery source/time, classification, factual basis, uncertainties, information barriers, required reviewers, status, disposition reference, review/expiry dates, and audit history. Layer 09 records and routes; Layer 06 and other competent authorities own assessment and disposition.

Unknown or Material unresolved conflicts block affected association, staffing proposal, information use, reuse, communication, and external action. An information barrier is a control proposal, not proof of effectiveness or permission.

## Pressure events

Requests to omit, soften, delay, reclassify, suppress, selectively communicate, or bypass negative facts, dissent, risk, QA findings, limitations, professional review, or contradictory evidence are recorded verbatim or by faithful reference with actor, time, affected records, response, and escalation.

Commercial or relationship considerations cannot close the event or change upstream truth. Retaliation risk, urgency, or Human Principal preference cannot remove the audit trail.

## Human Principal decisions

Layer 09 may request and record a Human Principal decision but cannot appoint, authenticate, impersonate, predict, infer, batch, delegate, reserve, replay, or substitute for the Human Principal. A title, CRM role, account owner, contact designation, agent instruction, model output, template, default, timeout, or prior pattern cannot become a proxy.

Valid decisions require verified authority from Layer 01, exact object and version, decision category, permitted options, stated outcome, scope, conditions, rationale/reference, effective time, expiry/revocation status, and immutable audit reference. Silence, elapsed time, attendance, informal sentiment, or technical success is not approval.

Human authority remains bounded by law, contract, `00_CORE`, certified truth-preservation rules, and non-waivable professional duties. A Human Principal may decide a governed operational question but cannot make false evidence true, erase dissent, rewrite QA history, or manufacture professional validation.
