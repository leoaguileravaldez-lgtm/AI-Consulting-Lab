# Exceptions and Professional Review

## Exception verification

QA verifies the canonical exception ID/version, policy/control, waivability, engagement, exact affected objects/versions, scope, rationale, alternatives, risks, compensating controls, monitoring, owner, effective date, expiry, revocation, consumption, Human Principal approval, and audit lineage.

QA cannot create, request on another actor's behalf, approve, reject, waive, extend, renew, inherit, broaden, reinterpret, consume, or close an exception. Silence, precedent, template language, similar engagements, or prior approval never create one. Expired, revoked, scope-mismatched, object-mismatched, unauthorized, or ambiguous exceptions are ineffective. Non-waivable controls remain non-waivable.

## Professional-review boundary

For legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, engineering, medical, safety, cybersecurity, audit, valuation, and other qualified or licensed domains, QA may verify:

- whether review was required;
- reviewer identity and governed qualification/license metadata;
- licensing jurisdiction and currency where applicable;
- independence and conflict metadata;
- scope and questions reviewed;
- exact object/version/hash;
- review and as-of dates;
- documented outcome/reference, limitations, and conditions;
- authenticity, provenance, expiry, and audit references.

QA does not determine substantive professional correctness, infer a missing conclusion, remove qualifications, broaden scope, impersonate a professional, or represent AI work as qualified review. Fake, unverifiable, expired, wrong-jurisdiction, wrong-scope, or stale evidence results in `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `NOT_READY` and canonical notification.
