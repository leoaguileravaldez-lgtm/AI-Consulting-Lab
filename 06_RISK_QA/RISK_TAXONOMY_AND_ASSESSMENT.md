# Risk Taxonomy and Assessment

## No parallel taxonomy

`06` references the canonical risk record and Material/Critical semantics in `00_CORE` and `01_ORCHESTRATOR/RECORD_SCHEMAS_AND_AUDIT.md`. It does not create new risk categories, likelihood scales, impact scales, confidence grades, or Critical thresholds.

For each exact task or recommendation, QA tests whether all plausible applicable canonical categories were assessed and whether cause, likelihood, impact, inherent risk, controls, mitigation, residual risk, owner, and acceptance authority are present and mutually coherent.

QA may identify an omitted category or recommend upward classification review. It may not lower classification or resolve ambiguity. Unknown impact, likelihood, category, or applicability uses the higher plausible treatment and routes to canonical review.

## Separated dimensions

The following remain separate and visible:

1. inherent risk;
2. control design;
3. control operating effectiveness;
4. residual risk;
5. uncertainty;
6. evidence strength owned by `04`;
7. analytical confidence under certified controls;
8. challenge status owned by `05`;
9. QA result;
10. professional-review requirement;
11. canonical workflow/readiness state;
12. Human Principal decision and risk acceptance.

No dimension is derived from another by default. No combined score may replace them.

## Diagnostic scoring

If future diagnostic scoring is used, every formula, input, scale, weight, limitation, version, and author must be transparent and reproducible. Scoring occurs only after categorical veto evaluation and cannot grant authority or hide an unresolved issue.
