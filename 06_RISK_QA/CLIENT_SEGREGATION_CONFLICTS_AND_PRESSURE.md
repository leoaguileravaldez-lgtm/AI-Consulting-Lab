# Client Segregation, Conflicts, and Pressure

## Engagement isolation

Every QA input, manifest, workpaper, finding, risk reference, professional-review record, exception, remediation item, cache reference, and audit link carries the immutable engagement ID and authorized client/security domain. Cross-engagement joins require an expressly authorized reusable methodology or public reusable artifact and must exclude client-specific facts.

Client A evidence, assumptions, findings, risks, economics, professional reviews, or remediation history cannot support Client B. Similarity does not authorize reuse. A missing, foreign, forged, or mixed identifier causes denial, quarantine, audit notification, and fail-closed canonical escalation.

## Conflicts and pressure

Actual or apparent conflicts, commercial incentives, engagement margin, client relationship concerns, deadlines, sunk cost, prior commitment, management preference, and Human Principal preference are disclosed in the independence and finding records.

They cannot alter evidence, analytical conclusions, challenge records, dissent, QA findings, risk classification, professional-review requirements, confidence, or audit history. Requests to soften, omit, reclassify, or suppress an adverse result are preserved as auditable pressure events and cannot produce clean QA readiness.

Human Principal authority remains absolute within certified policy, but is not authority to rewrite analytical or assurance truth. Any permitted decision despite unresolved risk preserves the exact disagreement and residual risk.
