# QA Independence and Authority

## Assignment

Every QA reviewer must hold an active, engagement-scoped canonical `RISK_REVIEWER` assignment or other exact eligible assignment established by `01_ORCHESTRATOR`. `06` cannot appoint, route, substitute, or qualify a reviewer.

The assignment and independence record identify actor and session/model, role, scope, reporting path, incentives, conflicts, prior participation, source/context exposure, control operation, remediation involvement, exception involvement, and audit references.

## Prohibited self-assurance

An actor may not be the sole QA verifier of its own:

- primary analysis or recommendation;
- evidence selection or validation;
- independent challenge or dissent disposition;
- control design or operation;
- remediation design or execution;
- exception request, ownership, approval, monitoring, or interpretation;
- professional conclusion;
- prior QA assessment where independent re-performance is required.

Different role labels, sessions, prompts, or model names do not establish independence where identity, provenance, method, assumptions, context, incentives, reporting path, or prior participation remain materially correlated.

## Mechanical independence result

Independence dimensions are tested separately and recorded as `PASS`, `LIMITED`, `FAIL`, or `UNKNOWN`. `UNKNOWN` is not a pass. Material/Critical work requires the independence level prescribed by certified policy. An unacceptable limitation, failed test, identity substitution, curated-only review packet, collusion indicator, or unresolvable correlation produces `NOT_ASSESSABLE` and an advisory impact notice.

## Authority separation

Risk owner, mitigation owner, QA verifier, professional reviewer, exception approver, and risk acceptor are distinct accountable references. Role combination is permitted only when certified policy allows it and all qualification, authority, conflict, and independence tests pass. Performing QA grants none of these other authorities.

The Human Principal retains non-delegable decisions and permitted residual-risk acceptance. Human preference cannot edit evidence, analysis, challenge, QA findings, risk classification, or audit history.
