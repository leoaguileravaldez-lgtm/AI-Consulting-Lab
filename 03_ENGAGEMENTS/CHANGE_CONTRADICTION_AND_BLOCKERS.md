# Change, Contradiction and Blocker Control

## Change Records

Any material change to scope, objective, decision question, assumption, target outcome, materiality, jurisdiction, deliverable, routing need, date, exposure, risk tolerance, data classification, tool, audience or measurement baseline creates an append-only change record before affected reliance. The same rule applies to evidence validity, provenance, source, version, freshness, interpretation, correction, revocation, expiry, supersession or invalidation.

The record identifies old/new values, proposer, reason, time, source, materiality, affected objects, approval need, decision, effective version and audit linkage. `03` records and projects impact; `01_ORCHESTRATOR` owns reclassification, approvals, routing and state transitions.

## Mechanical Downstream Invalidation

On an accepted Material change, traverse explicit dependency links from the changed object to every dependent question, assumption, evidence item, model, output, contradiction, synthesis, gate packet, approval, deliverable, KPI, benefit claim and closure check. Mark each affected projection `INVALIDATED` or `REVALIDATION_REQUIRED`, preserve the prior version, and notify `01` for the applicable canonical rework/block route.

A Material change to evidence validity, provenance, freshness or interpretation is itself a Material dependency change. Authoritative correction, revocation, expiry, supersession or invalidation triggers propagation even when an engagement-level change record did not enumerate evidence provenance as its changed field. Detection cannot wait for discretionary acceptance before affected reliance is suspended.

Invalidation is mechanical, not discretionary. Affected approvals are unusable because the evidence basis or exact object/version changed. Unaffected items remain usable only with a recorded dependency test. Restoration requires fresh source records, independent revalidation and approvals applicable to the new versions.

## Contradictions

Every contradiction preserves each proposition, owner, evidence, assumptions, confidence, materiality, dependency impact and proposed resolution. No synthesis may average, omit, relabel or manufacture consensus. Material contradictions require independent challenge/validation, the applicable source-conflict or policy/risk route, and explicit Human Principal visibility.

If Strategy says GO, Finance says REVISE, Operations says HOLD, Regulatory reports material uncertainty and Market evidence is incomplete, all five positions remain discrete. Evidence insufficiency blocks affected reliance; assurance tests independence and source correlation; synthesis presents conditional options; the Human Principal may decide only through canonical controls and cannot make absent evidence appear validated.

## Blockers

`03` references only canonical `01` blocker types and states. It may not invent a state. Each blocker records owner, scope, trigger, affected dependencies, remediation route, freshness and escalation. Deadlines, client pressure and apparent delivery success cannot close a Material blocker.

New contradictions found after synthesis invalidate the synthesis and downstream packet/deliverable projections, preserve prior versions, and route to existing remediation/validation/synthesis controls. Human Principal unavailability yields `BLOCKED_AUTHORIZATION` or the applicable hold; it never transfers authority.
