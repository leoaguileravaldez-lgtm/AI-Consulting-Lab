# Measurement, Closure and Engagement Economics

## Measurement Boundary

Measurement/KPI/Benefits Realization is the certified transversal capability in `02_SPECIALISTS`; `03_ENGAGEMENTS` only records planning references, applicability, implementation milestones, outcome links and closure readiness. Evidence Validation validates baseline/result evidence; Independent Challenge tests gaming and causality; Risk/QA reviews control integrity; only the Human Principal grants applicable exact approvals. No party self-certifies its Material result.

Measurement is mandatory for outcome-producing Material/Critical recommendations and proportionate where Routine outcomes matter. It may be `NOT_APPLICABLE` for descriptive, discovery, historical or issue-spotting work with no implemented outcome, only with reason, reviewer, impact and required approval. Deliverable production is never proof of success.

## Lifecycle

1. Define candidate baseline, KPI, owner, method, source/provenance, target, date, leading/lagging indicators and attribution limits.
2. Independently validate baseline integrity; lock the approved version before results are known.
3. Link financial/non-financial benefits, implementation milestones, assumptions, dependencies, confidence and unintended-consequence indicators.
4. Record actuals through new linked canonical follow-up tasks; never reopen terminal tasks.
5. Preserve adverse outcomes, variance, limitations, double-count tests and corrective-action recommendations.
6. Challenge/validate attribution and present results to the Human Principal. Recommendations do not authorize correction or progression.

Retroactive baseline or target manipulation, cherry-picked windows, selective reporting, correlation-as-causation, invented KPIs, double counting and suppression of failure invalidate the affected claim and fail closed.

## Closure Readiness

An engagement is ready for canonical `CLOSE_REVIEW` only when all canonical tasks are terminal or explicitly superseded; no pending, blocked, invalidated, revalidation-required or required-follow-up task is hidden; delivery status is known and governed; decisions/approvals and audit links are complete; Material contradictions are resolved or explicitly disclosed; required measurement and benefit-realization work is complete or measurement is validly N/A; retention/security/access duties are satisfied; and residual risks/incidents have accountable owners. Optional monitoring may be scheduled separately, but scheduling cannot satisfy a required measurement or follow-up obligation.

Closure readiness does not close anything. `01` owns closure transition and the required Human Principal authorization. A later optional or newly authorized measurement result links to the closed engagement through a new task and preserves the original record; it does not rewrite success or reopen a terminal task. A measurement follow-up already required for engagement closure must be completed before closure readiness can be true.

## Internal Engagement Economics

`03` may hold a restricted internal planning envelope: estimated and actual effort, approved limit/budget reference, specialist activation cost, research/external expert/delivery-preparation cost, forecast to complete, variance, reason, approval reference and as-of date.

Access is least-privilege, classified separately from client analysis, and auditable. Material budget/scope changes require Human Principal approval under canonical exact-object controls. The envelope cannot authorize spend, contract, staffing, pricing or external action.

Engagement economics and commercial pressure cannot influence analytical conclusions, evidence interpretation, confidence, uncertainty, dissent, specialist conclusions, validation results, risk classification, regulatory conclusions, measurement results, benefit attribution or recommendation content. Commercial attractiveness cannot upgrade evidence quality or confidence. Commercial considerations may inform separately authorized administrative decisions about staffing, scheduling, engagement economics, contracting, pricing or scope negotiation, but cannot reduce mandatory specialist participation, evidence thresholds, assurance, challenge, Risk/QA or disclosure.

When commercial pressure conflicts with analytical evidence, analytical integrity prevails. The pressure, conflict, requested alteration and disposition remain visible and auditable. Insufficient resources route to the applicable canonical capacity, scope or Human Principal decision and fail closed; they do not alter analytical truth or suppress an unfavorable conclusion.

Client-business valuation, capital allocation and financial feasibility remain with `SP_FINANCE_VALUATION_CAPITAL`. Pricing, billing, receivables, detailed margin and CRM belong to future commercial/client operations. These datasets use distinct identifiers and may be compared only through an authorized, explicit mapping.
