# Engagement Intake and Problem Definition

## Intake Boundary

Intake assembles a request for canonical `01_ORCHESTRATOR` conflict review, engagement authorization and classification. A provisional engagement ID permits intake/conflict preparation only; it grants no specialist access or analytical authority.

## Minimum Completeness

Analysis may not begin until canonical records establish:

- immutable engagement ID, requestor and intended decision-maker;
- request, intended use, audience, decision deadline and requested deliverables;
- scope, exclusions, jurisdictions, data classification, authorized sources/tools and retention;
- materiality, financial/regulatory/technology/public-sector/workforce exposures and reversibility;
- constraints, known risks, conflicts, available evidence, gaps and uncertainty;
- required rigor, candidate capability needs, assurance needs, approver and authorization reference.

Missing or ambiguous engagement, purpose, scope, authority, data, tier, limit, approver or conflict information remains in the applicable canonical intake/block state. Premature analysis is prohibited.

## Problem-Definition Contract

The contract keeps six fields separate:

1. `REQUEST`: the client/Human Principal wording, uncorrected and attributed.
2. `OBSERVED_CONDITION`: evidence-linked observations, not causes.
3. `ROOT_PROBLEM_HYPOTHESES`: testable alternatives with assumptions and disconfirmation criteria.
4. `DECISION_TO_SUPPORT`: the exact choice, authority, timing and consequences.
5. `SUCCESS_CRITERIA`: decision quality and, where applicable, outcome measures.
6. `OUT_OF_SCOPE`: explicit exclusions and known adjacent questions.

## Deliverable-to-Decision Fit

The case must state whether the requested artifact can support the actual decision. A business plan, model, strategy or assessment with insufficient evidence, wrong scope or no decision relevance is flagged `FIT_GAP`. Material gaps block plan readiness until the Human Principal resolves scope through canonical approval/change control; the system does not silently reinterpret the request.

## Intake Challenge

Intake records thesis provenance, client preferences, contrary evidence, conflicts, stakeholder incentives, political/institutional constraints and alternative problem framings. Respectful evidence-based challenge is required where a preferred thesis may bias framing, but challenge never overrides Human Principal authority.
