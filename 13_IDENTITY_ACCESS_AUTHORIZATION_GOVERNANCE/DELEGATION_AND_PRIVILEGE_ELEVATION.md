# Delegation and Privilege Elevation

## Delegation

Delegation is an explicit record binding delegator/delegate effective actors, source authority, permitted authority/permission subset, exact actions/objects, client/entity/engagement/jurisdiction, purpose, confidentiality, effective/expiry time, revocation, conditions, audit, and transitivity.

Default is `NON_TRANSITIVE`. Further delegation requires an explicit source grant permitting exact depth, delegate classes, scope, and duration. No delegation may exceed, change category, outlive, or cross the source authority. Non-delegable Human Principal authority, mandatory professional authority, SOD, isolation, confidentiality, provenance, or legal constraints cannot be delegated away.

Unknown delegate, chain gap, scope amplification, circular chain, alias laundering, expired/revoked link, cross-boundary transfer, or absent source authority denies the entire affected chain. Delegation Revocation takes precedence over all cached or copied descendants.

## Privilege elevation

Elevation is exceptional, separately requested and decided, exact-purpose/action/object/boundary scoped, approved by an eligible distinct effective actor, time-limited, revocable, audited, and automatically architecturally expired at its recorded deadline. It cannot become permanent through renewal, recurrence, retry, silence, or continued session.

Requester cannot approve their own elevation. Administrative elevation does not grant business, professional, evidence, QA, release, risk, compliance, decision, workflow, Human, or external-action authority.
