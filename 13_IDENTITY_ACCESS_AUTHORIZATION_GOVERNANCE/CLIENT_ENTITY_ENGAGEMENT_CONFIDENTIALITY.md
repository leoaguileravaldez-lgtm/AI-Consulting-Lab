# Client, Entity, Engagement, and Confidentiality Boundaries

Every client-bound access path requires the exact Layer 10 client instance/security domain, Layer 09 client, legal entity, Layer 03 engagement/subdomain, jurisdiction, purpose, classification, confidentiality, object set, and operation. A valid relation in one dimension never supplies another.

Same-client wrong-engagement, parent/subsidiary, subsidiary/parent, affiliate, branch, related party, similar name, shared ownership, personnel, advisor, or email domain never transfers access. Copied records, caches, embeddings, summaries, derived artifacts, exports, search, and retrieval preserve all source boundaries and require independent authorization.

Confidentiality labels reference certified canonical classifications, including Public, Internal, Client Confidential, Restricted, and the applicable highest Material category. Layer 13 does not redefine or downgrade them. Higher privilege in one domain does not grant another. Missing, malformed, conflicting, or implicitly downgraded classification denies access.

Boundary and confidentiality changes revoke affected prior decisions and require exact revalidation. Cross-client, cross-entity, cross-engagement, cross-jurisdiction, or cross-purpose movement defaults to deny even when technically reachable.
