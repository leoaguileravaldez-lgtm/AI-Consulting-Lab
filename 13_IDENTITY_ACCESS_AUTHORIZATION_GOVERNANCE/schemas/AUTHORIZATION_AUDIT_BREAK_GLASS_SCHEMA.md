Every `ordered_deputy_chain_entries` item physically contains sequence number, predecessor/successor, identity/principal/effective-actor ID/version/hash, identity class, actor and on-behalf-of references, role assignments, permission and authority grants, delegation source and exact link, object/action scope, purpose, client/entity/engagement, jurisdiction, confidentiality, authentication/context, lifecycle/freshness, effective/expiry/revocation/supersession, validation, and audit references. The originator is sequence zero; every intermediary is ordered; the final executor is explicit.

`request_authorization_comparison_manifest` is a typed, exhaustive, per-field comparison of the exact Access Request against this decision: request identity/version/hash; requester and originator; chain count, order, each member and predecessor/successor; final executor; roles; permissions; authorities; delegation sources; objects/actions; purpose; jurisdiction; boundaries; confidentiality; lifecycle; freshness; validity; and constraints. Each comparison stores requested value/hash, authorized value/hash, and result `EXACT`, `FAIL_CLOSED_NARROWING`, or `MISMATCH`. Narrowing requires a new explicit authorization lifecycle and may never omit, replace, reorder, or substitute a member or executor. Any missing comparison, mismatch, stale version, unverifiable hash, chain mutation, identity uncertainty, or expansion makes the decision non-approved and non-actionable.

Direct access requires `deputy_chain_count = 0`, an explicitly empty chain, and a typed `zero_deputy_identity_equality_manifest` proving requester actor/principal/effective actor = originator actor/principal/effective actor = final executing actor/principal/effective actor = authoritative Authorization Decision actor. The equality is over canonical resolved IDs, versions, and hashes. Any differing human, alias, shared account, service, model, agent, machine, proxy, or intermediary requires a non-empty validated chain; otherwise deny.

The chain is invalid if any member or link is missing, duplicated, aliased ambiguously, shared without attribution, circular, unauthorized, stale, expired, revoked, superseded, cross-boundary, or unresolved. No member inherits another member's authority. Effective authorization is the least-privilege intersection of all applicable chain-member permissions, authorities, delegations, scopes, purposes, jurisdictions, client/entity/engagement boundaries, confidentiality constraints, validity states, and non-waivable controls. Empty or mismatched intersection denies. Privilege/scope/purpose/jurisdiction/boundary amplification, confidentiality downgrade, model/agent substitution, Human Principal proxying, and delegation self-approval are prohibited.

Each manifest entry includes exact ID/version/hash, owner/source authority, client/entity/engagement/jurisdiction, purpose, confidentiality, lifecycle, freshness, expiry/revocation, and validation. Missing is not empty; legitimate empty categories require exact category/reason/scope/authority validation. `APPROVED` requires a complete current deputy chain, validated least-privilege intersection, complete current input reconciliation, explicit valid Permission Grant, Authority Grant when determination authority is needed, all boundaries/purpose/confidentiality/SOD/time dependencies passing, and required Human reference. Otherwise deny.

`chain_completeness_manifest` deterministically compares Access Request → canonical identity resolution → every ordered delegation/deputy transition → Authorization Decision → Access Decision projection → Break-Glass Decision when applicable → exact execution instance → emergency consumption/replay state when applicable → final executor → outcome → audit event. It stores expected and observed counts, ordinals, exact member and record IDs/versions/hashes, predecessor/successor links, delegation sources, effective permission/authority at each transition, request/decision reconciliation hashes, authoritative decision hashes, Authorization/Access equality, Break-Glass/Access/execution equality, use identifier and consumption reference, final-executor reconciliation, and zero-deputy equality proof when applicable. Truncation, omission, substitution, reordering, duplication, alias ambiguity, unresolved identity, broken adjacency, independent assembly, replay, or internal inconsistency invalidates authorization and the event. The audit subject cannot be sole audit authority/verifier. Events are append-only by architecture.

A Break-Glass Decision is a subordinate, non-authoritative, single-use transformation of exactly one existing current canonical Authorization Decision and its exact reconciled Access Decision; it is never a parallel authorization path. `authority_source_type` must equal `CANONICAL_AUTHORIZATION_DECISION`, `direct_canonical_authorization_source_ref` must equal the listed Authorization Decision ID/version/hash, `parent_emergency_decision_ref` must be validated empty, `emergency_delegation_depth` must be zero, and `nested_emergency_validation_state` must be `NO_EMERGENCY_DERIVED_AUTHORITY`. A generic authority, another emergency decision, emergency delegation, recursive/nested chain, or Break-Glass-created Break-Glass authority denies.

`break_glass_access_execution_reconciliation_manifest` is a typed, exhaustive, contemporaneous comparison across the canonical Authorization Decision, its Access Decision projection, the Break-Glass Decision, and the exact execution instance. It compares authorization-chain ID; record IDs/versions/hashes; actor/effective actor; final executor; client/entity/engagement; object/action; purpose; jurisdiction; confidentiality; boundaries; lifecycle/freshness; delegation/deputy chain; state; validity; revocation/supersession; and execution instance. Every result must be `EXACT` or explicitly narrower within `canonical_emergency_least_privilege_intersection`; actor, executor, chain, and execution instance permit no narrowing or substitution. Missing, mismatched, stale, revoked, expired, superseded, post-hoc, or unreconciled input denies.

Emergency use is single-use: `emergency_authorization_use_id` is unique within the authorization chain and binds exactly one execution instance; `permitted_use_count` must equal one. Before use, `consumed_use_count = 0`, `consumption_state = UNCONSUMED`, and `replay_state = NOT_REPLAYED`. The sole use creates an append-only consumption event and immutable successor record version with `consumed_use_count = 1`, `consumption_state = CONSUMED`, `consumed_at`, and `immutable_consumption_audit_event_ref`; the original is never overwritten. Once consumed, any further attempt—including within the validity window—has `replay_state = REPLAY_REJECTED` and is non-actionable. Duplicate use ID, altered execution binding, missing consumption reconciliation, count outside zero-or-one, or unknown prior-use state denies. Validity/freshness/expiry do not substitute for replay protection.

Its canonical decision, Access Decision, input manifest, request comparison, complete deputy chain, effective actor, final executor, object/action scope, purpose, jurisdiction, confidentiality, and boundaries must reconcile exactly. Emergency transformations must be enumerated and produce the least-privilege intersection with—not expansion of—the canonical authorization and Access Decision. Break-glass cannot create authority, change an actor/executor/chain, bypass reconciliation or SOD/Human controls, waive non-waivable controls, or self-approve. It grants no business or external-action authority.
