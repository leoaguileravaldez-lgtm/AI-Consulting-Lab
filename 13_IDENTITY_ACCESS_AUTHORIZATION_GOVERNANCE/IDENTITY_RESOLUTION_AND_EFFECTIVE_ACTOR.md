# Identity Resolution and Effective Actor

## Deterministic resolution

Resolution binds every presented identifier to exact Identity and Principal records using attributable verification references, issuer, lifecycle, effective window, aliases, account ownership, employment/contract/client relationship, delegation, and contradictions. Email similarity, domain, display name, CRM contact, shared personnel, title, or metadata cannot establish identity.

Multiple emails/accounts, renamed accounts, aliases, shared accounts, service accounts, delegated accounts, models, agents, contractors, client users, related parties, duplicates, stale identities, and terminated/suspended identities remain explicit. Merges and splits create new versions and preserve every source and contradiction. Shared accounts are prohibited for Material attribution unless a certified exception preserves individual effective-actor attribution; otherwise deny.

## Effective actor

`effective_actor_id` is the ultimate accountable human or canonical machine authority controlling the act after resolving aliases, accounts, acting-on-behalf-of relationships, delegation chains, model/agent invocations, and common control. Nominally distinct identifiers do not establish distinct actors.

Resolution records the presented identity, canonical identity, principal, controller, every alias/delegation edge, source verification, validity, boundary, conflict, and confidence limitation. Unknown controller, circular delegation, alias collision, duplicate ambiguity, shared-account ambiguity, or identity-class conflict is `IDENTITY_BLOCKED`.

Terminated, suspended, compromised, expired, revoked, or superseded identity state overrides stale assignments, grants, sessions, caches, copies, and prior decisions.
