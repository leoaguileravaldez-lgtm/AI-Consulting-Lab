# Human Principal and Automation Boundary

Explicit Human Principal decision references are required where certified policy reserves Material exception disposition, non-delegable approval, risk acceptance, Material override request, substantive termination, or release authorization.

Layer 11 may request and record a decision reference but cannot appoint, authenticate, impersonate, infer, predict, delegate, batch, reserve, replay, or proxy Human Principal authority. No engine, model, agent, role, client request, workflow state, queue position, consensus, default, silence, timeout, retry, recurrence, or system expectation is approval.

Human approval cannot make unsupported evidence true, validate analysis, erase dissent, waive required professional review, suppress risk/QA, downgrade confidentiality, authorize cross-client leakage, or rewrite history.

Layer 11 defines interfaces only. It contains no executable code, scripts, runtime engine, scheduler, worker, queue implementation, daemon, connector, webhook, API call, email, messaging, credentials, secrets, tokens, or autonomous background process. Possession or future availability of capability never creates authority. Layer 12 remains separately governed.
