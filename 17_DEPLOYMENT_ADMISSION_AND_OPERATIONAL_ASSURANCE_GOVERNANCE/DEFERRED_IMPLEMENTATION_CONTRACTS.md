# Deferred Implementation Contracts

NORMATIVE_SOURCE: `CANONICAL_MODEL.json`  
NORMATIVE_SOURCE_VERSION: `1.0.0`  
DERIVATION_TYPE: Category 2 future-owner projection

The normative model allocates twelve implementation families. Every entry explicitly records future owner, rationale, prerequisites, required evidence, fail-closed absence behavior and future certification condition:

1. Reproducible build and provenance generation.
2. Artifact registry immutability, signing and SBOM production.
3. Configuration and dependency capture.
4. Environment identity and generation registry.
5. Security, privacy and isolation testing.
6. Reliability, capacity, failure and recovery testing.
7. Telemetry evidence production.
8. Identity, access and attestation mechanisms.
9. Deployment-authorization workflow implementation under Layer 01.
10. Deployment and rollback execution.
11. Post-deployment monitoring and operational control.
12. External-action execution and effect/outcome reconciliation.

These mechanisms are not present or certified. Absence never becomes evidence, readiness, eligibility, authorization or permission. Family 12 remains separately governed and unnumbered.
