# Compliance Obligation and Constraint Model

## Obligations

A Compliance Obligation records exact source instrument and authority, provision/version, jurisdiction, regulated entity, applicable activity/data/product, effective and expiration dates, mandatory/discretionary status, interpretation authority, evidence, professional review, dependencies, exceptions allowed, required controls, reporting/retention duties, conflicts, and limitations.

Client statements, CRM fields, summaries, retrieval, model output, or prior practice are not authoritative obligations. Legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, privacy, security, or other specialist interpretation remains with qualified humans under certified policy.

## Constraints

Jurisdiction, Policy, and Contractual Constraint objects remain distinct. Each binds exact legal entity, engagement, territory, source/version, applicability test, prohibited/required conditions, hierarchy, effective window, review authority, conflict references, and audit. Parent, subsidiary, affiliate, related party, branch, and similarly named entity are never interchangeable.

## Assessment and conflict hierarchy

A Compliance Assessment maps each applicable obligation and constraint to exact controls, evidence, gaps, uncertainties, dependencies, conflicts, remediation owner, due date, and conclusion status. `COMPLIANT` cannot be inferred from no known issue; incomplete scope is `UNKNOWN_BLOCKED`.

Applicable law and binding obligations control under prior certified governance. A hierarchy does not permit Layer 12 to resolve legal interpretation. Conflicting jurisdiction, policy, contract, or professional requirements stop the affected decision and route to canonical qualified authority and the Human Principal where required. The stricter applicable requirement governs pending resolution.
