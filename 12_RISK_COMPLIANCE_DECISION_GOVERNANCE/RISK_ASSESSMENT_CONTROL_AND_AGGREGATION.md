# Risk Assessment, Controls, and Aggregation

## Assessment and controls

Risk assessments bind exact Risk Record/version, method/version, certified evidence and analysis references, assessors and qualifications, dates, dependencies, uncertainty, contradictions, and limitations. Layer 12 records assessment governance; it cannot manufacture evidence or professional competence.

A Risk Control Reference points to an exact control owner, control object/version, intended risk response, coverage, operating period, implementation state, test/assurance reference, limitations, and dependencies. Design, implementation, operation, and effectiveness are distinct. Layer 06 assurance remains canonical where applicable.

Residual risk requires exact inherent assessment, exact credited controls, effectiveness support, remaining exposure, uncertainty, open dissent, and the applicable acceptance authority. Human Principal disposition cannot make an unsupported control effective.

## Scoring

Scores assist ordering only. Every component, scale, weight, method, version, range, assumption, and missing value remains visible. No averaging, weighting, confidence increase, or portfolio score can offset missing provenance, stale evidence, invalid authority, missing jurisdiction, unresolved conflict, invalid boundary, confidentiality breach, SOD violation, incomplete dependency, expired decision, or unauthorized waiver.

## Aggregation and concentration

Aggregation is prohibited by default across clients and engagements. Within one authorized boundary it requires compatible methods, units, horizons, non-duplicated lineage, explicit correlation assumptions, concentration analysis, and exact membership/version inventory. Aggregation never erases a Material component or substitutes a portfolio score for individual disposition.

Cross-client aggregation must first pass Layer 08 reuse governance and Layer 10 isolation controls, plus lawful client/Human authorization where required. De-identification, similarity, common ownership, parent/subsidiary status, or commercial reporting alone cannot authorize it. Unknown correlation or entity relationship fails closed.
