# Cross-Layer Authority and Interfaces

Layer 12 accepts immutable exact-version references only. It produces advisory risk/compliance records, gate results, formal decision-control records, and escalation references for canonical owners. It does not write another layer's state.

- Layer 00 policies, materiality, approval categories, permissions, and Human controls remain controlling.
- Layer 01 validates canonical roles, approvals, audit, and task transitions; Layer 12 cannot transition state.
- Layer 02 retains analysis and professional judgment.
- Layer 03 retains engagement identity, scope, and lifecycle.
- Layer 04 remains exclusive evidence/provenance/freshness authority.
- Layer 05 retains challenge and dissent authority.
- Layer 06 retains risk/QA assurance and professional-review outputs; Layer 12 cannot mark QA passed.
- Layer 07 retains deliverable readiness, release, publication, and delivery authority.
- Layer 08 retains knowledge/reuse authorization.
- Layer 09 retains client-operations state.
- Layer 10 retains client instantiation and isolation.
- Layer 11 retains workflow-control proposals and canonical Layer 01 transition references; Layer 12 does not execute decisions.

An interface reference carries object ID/version/hash, owner layer, purpose, scope, boundary, status, freshness, expiry/revocation, confidentiality, limitations, and audit. Retrieval or possession is not authority. Unknown ownership, conflicting state, unavailable validation, or canonical drift blocks reliance.

Decision input snapshots preserve these exact references without copying or reinterpreting canonical authority. Layer 04 remains evidence authority; Layer 06 retains certified risk/QA review authority; Layer 07 retains release authority; Layer 11 retains execution/workflow authority. A reconciled or approved Layer 12 Decision Record still cannot execute, transition, release, validate evidence, mark QA passed, or proxy Human Principal authority.
