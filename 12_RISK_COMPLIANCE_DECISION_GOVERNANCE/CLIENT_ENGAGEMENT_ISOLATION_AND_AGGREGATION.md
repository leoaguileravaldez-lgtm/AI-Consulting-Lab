# Client and Engagement Isolation

Every client-bound risk, assessment, exposure, control reference, obligation, constraint, gate, request, decision, approval, escalation, exception, waiver, conflict, and SOD record carries Layer 10 client instance/security domain, Layer 09 client ID, exact legal entity, Layer 03 engagement, engagement subdomain, jurisdiction, purpose, classification, and confidentiality.

Cross-client and cross-engagement copying, substitution, joins, aggregation, comparison, search, retrieval, prompt/context use, embeddings, caches, summaries, exports, derived artifacts, approvals, decisions, exceptions, waivers, or conflicts default deny. Shared physical infrastructure may not remove logical boundaries or expose metadata.

Parent, subsidiary, affiliate, branch, related party, prospect, former client, and similarly named entity require explicit canonical relationship and applicability records. Common ownership or prior work does not transfer authority or information. Ambiguous, duplicate, stale, changed, or contradictory identity/state causes quarantine and revalidation.

Copied assessments and templates lose all client-specific validity. Reuse must pass Layer 08 and Layer 10; approval and waiver records are never reusable across boundaries. Every permitted aggregation preserves exact membership, authorization, provenance, anti-reidentification controls, limitations, and ability to trace Material components without exposing them improperly.
