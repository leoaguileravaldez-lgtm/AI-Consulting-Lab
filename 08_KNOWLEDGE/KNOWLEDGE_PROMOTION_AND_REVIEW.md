# Knowledge Promotion and Review

## Record semantics

`OBSERVATION`, `PATTERN_CANDIDATE`, `VALIDATION_REQUIRED`, `REUSABLE`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `STALE`, `SUPERSEDED`, `DEPRECATED` and `QUARANTINED` are knowledge-record metadata only. They cannot alter engagement state, create/resolve blockers, validate evidence, alter challenge/QA, approve recommendations, accept risk, grant access or authorize reuse by themselves.

## Promotion separation

The originator, candidate owner, sanitization producer, sanitization reviewer, generalization producer, generalization reviewer, evidence validator, challenge reviewer, QA reviewer and reuse authorizer remain separately attributable. Existing certified Human/approval authority authorizes Material reuse; Layer 08 creates no `KNOWLEDGE_APPROVER`.

The originating specialist, analyst/model, engagement owner, sanitization producer or generalization producer cannot be the sole Material reuse validator. Independence records actor, model/session, context, source selection, method, assumptions, incentives, reporting path and prior participation. Unknown or failed independence prevents promotion.

Promotion requires lineage, scope, evidence support, contradiction review, negative cases, de-identification/generalization review, applicable challenge/QA/professional review, reuse policy, authorization and audit linkage. Repetition alone is insufficient.
