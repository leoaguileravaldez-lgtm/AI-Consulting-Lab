# Knowledge Architecture

## Purpose and authority

`08_KNOWLEDGE` preserves explicit, versioned, auditable methodology, public knowledge, patterns, lessons, failures, benchmarks, taxonomies, templates and institutional know-how for governed reuse. It is subordinate to `00_CORE` and the canonical workflow, evidence, challenge, QA, deliverable, approval, security and audit controls of Layers 01 through 07.

Core invariants:

- Reuse methodology where authorized and applicable.
- Reuse factual conclusions only while provenance, applicability, freshness, scope, client boundary and authorization remain valid.
- Past success is evidence to investigate, not authority to conclude.
- Untraceable model memory is not institutional knowledge.
- A knowledge object is not an evidence object or canonical truth.

`08` may assemble candidates, preserve lineage, test reuse criteria, maintain knowledge-record metadata and publish advisory impact notices. It cannot perform analysis; create or validate evidence; alter challenge, QA, risk, deliverable or professional records; approve promotion or recommendations; accept risk; change state or blockers; grant access; act as CRM; execute tools; or act as a Human Principal proxy.

If any Layer 08 rule conflicts with a certified rule, the certified rule controls and affected reuse fails closed.

## Layer ownership

`01_ORCHESTRATOR` owns workflow, assignments, approvals, state, blockers and audit authority. `04_RESEARCH_EVIDENCE` owns sources and evidence. `05` owns challenge. `06` owns QA/risk control. `07` owns presentation. `08` owns reusable knowledge records only. Future Client/CRM, identity/access, storage/retrieval and Automation/Connector modules remain separate.
