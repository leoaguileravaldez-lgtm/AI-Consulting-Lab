# Contradiction and Duplication Control

Conflicting knowledge remains separate and linked. Contradiction records preserve propositions and versions, sources/root lineage, scope, jurisdiction, sector, population, period, sample, methodology, assumptions, professional review, challenge, limitations, applicability and unresolved uncertainty.

No automatic merge, averaging, voting, ranking suppression, confidence aggregation, newest-record preference or narrative consensus may erase disagreement. Resolution creates a new version and preserves prior positions.

Deduplication uses provenance, not text similarity alone. Objects sharing source, dataset, client, author, model, prompt, template, method or institutional dependency remain correlated. Consolidation may improve retrieval but cannot increase corroboration or delete distinct limitations and history.

The number of records, citations, retrievals or uses never proves truth or independence.
