# Professional Knowledge Boundary

Professional conclusions remain bound to exact object, reviewed version, jurisdiction, scope, date/as-of date, reviewer identity, qualification status, limitations, reliance conditions and expiration/re-review trigger.

Layer 08 may preserve authorized professional methodology, public guidance, qualification requirements and documented review references. It cannot provide current legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, engineering, medical, safety, audit, valuation, cybersecurity or other licensed judgment; reuse conclusions outside scope; infer continuing qualification; remove limitations; or convert methodology into certification.

Changed or unknown scope, jurisdiction, qualification, version or freshness produces `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

Human Principal preference or knowledge promotion cannot create professional validity.

Distribution labels such as `PUBLIC`, `INTERNAL`, `RESTRICTED`, `CLIENT_BOUND` and `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` are metadata only. They do not authenticate, authorize, grant access, publish, share or transmit.
