# Reuse Eligibility and Applicability

Every reuse requires an exact receiving-engagement decision record. Previous eligibility never establishes current validity.

Required checks include object type/version, intended use, record status, source provenance, evidence status, freshness, jurisdiction, sector/subsector, scale/population, period, scope, methodology compatibility, client boundary, confidentiality, de-identification, generalization, professional dependency, contradictions, limitations, challenge/QA requirements, supersession and exact reuse authorization.

Advisory results are:

- `ELIGIBLE_AS_METHODOLOGY`;
- `ELIGIBLE_AS_BACKGROUND`;
- `ELIGIBLE_AS_HYPOTHESIS`;
- `ELIGIBLE_AS_BENCHMARK_CANDIDATE`;
- `EVIDENCE_REVALIDATION_REQUIRED`;
- `APPLICABILITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED`;
- `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`;
- `CLIENT_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`;
- `NOT_REUSABLE`;
- `UNKNOWN`.

These are knowledge-record results only. They do not create state, approval, evidence validation, confidence or permission. `UNKNOWN` is non-reusable.

Receiving-engagement injection classes are `METHODOLOGY`, `BACKGROUND_CONTEXT`, `BENCHMARK_CANDIDATE`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `EXPERIENCE_PATTERN` and `REUSABLE_PUBLIC_FACT`. Each retains its class and cannot bypass evidence, applicability, challenge, QA, professional-review or Human-decision requirements.
