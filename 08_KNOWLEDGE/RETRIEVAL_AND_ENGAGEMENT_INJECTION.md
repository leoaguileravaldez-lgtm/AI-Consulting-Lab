# Retrieval and Engagement Injection

## Retrieval non-authority

`TOP RESULT ≠ TRUE`. `MOST SIMILAR ≠ APPLICABLE`. `MOST FREQUENT ≠ CORRECT`. `HIGHEST RETRIEVAL SCORE ≠ DECISION AUTHORITY`.

Rank, similarity, embedding proximity, frequency, usage, popularity, recency alone and model confidence are retrieval signals only. They cannot create evidence, confidence, applicability, authorization, recommendation or canonical state.

Future retrieval exposes object type/version, scope, freshness, applicability, lineage, limitations, contradictions, reuse restrictions, client boundary and professional dependency. Contradictory or restrictive results cannot be silently ranked away. Unknown identity, lineage, boundary, freshness or applicability is non-clean.

## Injection

Every injection records receiving engagement, exact object/version, injection class, intended use, requester, freshness, applicability, client boundary, evidence/professional requirements, contradiction, compatibility, limitations, authorization and audit linkage.

No injection bypasses evidence validation, applicability, challenge, QA, professional review, Human decision or canonical workflow. Opaque model memory and “used before” are invalid injection grounds.
