# Knowledge Implementation Readiness

## Non-runtime boundary

This package is Markdown architecture only. It creates no storage, index, vector search, retrieval engine, browser, auto-refresh agent, webhook, connector, external retrieval, subscription, email, messaging, credential, token, background task or runtime execution authority. It is not CRM and owns no contacts, accounts, sales pipeline, opportunity stages, billing, prospecting, relationship history or client communication.

Institutional knowledge must be explicit, versioned, auditable, provenance-bound, scope-bound, reviewable, revocable and supersedable. Untraceable model memory cannot receive clean reusable status.

## Material veto

Missing or Material weakness in provenance, source status, freshness, applicability, independence, client segregation, professional review, contradiction, scope, method compatibility or authorization is non-compensable. No aggregate score, popularity, frequency, rank, Human preference or commercial pressure produces clean reuse.

## Residual limitations

Perfect de-identification cannot be guaranteed architecturally and re-identification risk cannot reach zero. Physical isolation requires future storage/access/index/cache/embedding controls. Retrieval correctness needs runtime testing. Freshness automation needs separately governed tooling. Applicability and benchmarks may require experts. Covert collusion cannot be eliminated by documentation. Professional qualification verification needs authoritative sources. Large graphs require operational tooling. Common origins may remain unknown when external provenance is opaque. Success/failure completeness depends on organizational reporting.
