# Benchmark Integrity

Every Material benchmark specifies numerator, denominator, metric definition, population, sample, sector/subsector, jurisdiction/geography, company size, period/as-of date, currency, nominal/real basis, accounting basis, percentile/statistic, source/evidence, methodology, exclusions, sponsorship/conflicts, limitations and applicability conditions.

Receiving-engagement comparison tests every dimension. `UNKNOWN` dimensional compatibility is non-clean and blocks Material comparison. Similar labels, popularity or prior use do not establish comparability.

Client-derived benchmarks remain client-bound unless independently supported beyond the originating client, effectively de-identified/generalized and exactly authorized. A client margin does not become an industry standard through aggregation or name removal.

Layer 08 records benchmark candidates and comparability assessments. It does not calculate new analytical benchmarks, validate evidence or authorize reliance.
