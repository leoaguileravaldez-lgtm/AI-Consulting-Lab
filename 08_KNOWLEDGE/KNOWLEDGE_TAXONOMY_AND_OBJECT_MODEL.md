# Knowledge Taxonomy and Object Model

## Classes

- `REUSABLE_METHODOLOGY`: authorized method or procedure; no factual authority by itself.
- `REUSABLE_PUBLIC_KNOWLEDGE`: public-information summary requiring current `04` evidence for Material factual use.
- `EXPERIENCE_PATTERN`: conditional recurring observation; not evidence or statistics by default.
- `CLIENT_SPECIFIC_FACT`: engagement-bound and non-reusable by default.
- `HYPOTHESIS_OR_HEURISTIC`: inquiry guidance without evidentiary authority.
- `TEMPLATE_OR_STRUCTURE`: reusable blank form without inherited client facts.
- `LESSON_LEARNED`: scoped interpretation of an outcome.
- `FAILURE_MEMORY`: governed negative outcome, defect or reversed conclusion.
- `BENCHMARK`: dimensionally defined candidate comparison.
- `PROFESSIONAL_METHOD_REFERENCE`: authorized methodology/guidance, not current professional advice.
- `DEPRECATED_OR_SUPERSEDED_KNOWLEDGE`: retained for audit, not ordinary active reuse.

Classes do not silently convert. Repetition cannot promote a hypothesis; experience cannot become evidence; name removal cannot convert a client fact into reusable knowledge.

## Object fields

Each object identifies knowledge ID/type/version, title/summary, domain, sector, jurisdiction, applicability scope, origin, originating engagement where applicable, client boundary, de-identification/generalization status, methodology version, freshness/review dates, validity, record status, supersession, limitations, assumptions, exceptions, lineage references, challenge/QA/professional references, authorization references, reuse policy, distribution metadata, actors and audit references.

Source quality, evidence validation, confidence, workflow, permission and professional conclusions remain referenced from their certified owners. Cached fields are non-authoritative.
