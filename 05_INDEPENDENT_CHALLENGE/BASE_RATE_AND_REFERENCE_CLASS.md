# Base Rate and Reference Class

## Purpose

Compare inside-view projections with relevant historical or external experience to expose exceptionalism, founder optimism, planning fallacy, selection bias and survivorship bias.

## Selection Protocol

1. Define the exact forecasted outcome and horizon.
2. Identify candidate classes before observing preferred results.
3. Record inclusion/exclusion criteria and search completeness.
4. Obtain evidence through `04_RESEARCH_EVIDENCE`.
5. Test similarity in sector, scale, maturity, jurisdiction, technology, regulation and implementation context.
6. disclose survivorship, publication, period, sponsor and data-quality bias.
7. compare inside and outside views with uncertainty.
8. explain any justified departure and its evidence.

Reference classes may use public reusable evidence only after engagement-specific applicability, freshness, jurisdiction and lineage review. Client-specific data never crosses engagement boundaries without canonical authorization.

`05` owns adversarial selection and interpretation; `02` may perform the forecast/calculation; `04` validates evidence. A base rate is not automatically applicable and cannot override direct engagement evidence merely by volume.
