# Challenge Taxonomy

## Core Methods

- `FALSIFICATION_TEST`: specify observations that would refute or materially weaken the target.
- `ALTERNATIVE_HYPOTHESIS`: construct credible competing explanations.
- `ASSUMPTION_ATTACK`: expose, test and bound explicit or hidden assumptions.
- `BOUNDARY_CONDITION_TEST`: test where the claimed relationship ceases to hold.
- `DOWNSIDE_SCENARIO`: test plausible adverse conditions.
- `EXTREME_BUT_PLAUSIBLE_SCENARIO`: test high-impact conditions without inventing absurd cases.
- `CONTRADICTION_ANALYSIS`: preserve and interpret incompatible claims/evidence.
- `DEPENDENCY_FAILURE`: test single, correlated and joint dependency failure.
- `PRE_MORTEM`: assume implementation failed and identify prior causes/signals.
- `RED_TEAM_ANALYSIS`: combine targeted methods against a Material decision.

## Shared Methods and Ownership

`MODEL_REPLICATION`, `SENSITIVITY_ANALYSIS`, `COUNTERFACTUAL`, `BASE_RATE_COMPARISON`, and `REFERENCE_CLASS_FORECAST` may be framed and interpreted by `05`. Governed calculations belong to `02` Quantitative Decision Science when invoked; evidence provenance belongs to `04`; formal validation belongs to the canonical `VALIDATOR`; future Risk/QA owns control compliance and residual-risk analysis.

A challenger-run calculation is a falsification artifact. It cannot substitute for formal validation, certify the model, or create quantitative authority.

## Outcomes

- `NO_MATERIAL_OBJECTION`
- `MATERIAL_WEAKNESS`
- `MATERIAL_CONTRADICTION`
- `REVALIDATION_REQUIRED`
- `RECOMMENDATION_FRAGILE`
- `RECOMMENDATION_ROBUST`
- `UNRESOLVED_DISSENT`

These are subordinate challenge-record findings, not canonical states, decisions, confidence levels, approvals, rejections, blocker dispositions, risk acceptance, delivery authorization, closure or transitions.

## Proportional Use

Routine work uses producer self-check or proportionate peer review unless triggered. Material work normally requires one independently assigned challenger and methods fit to the decision. Critical work requires multiple relevant methods, enhanced correlation controls and qualified human review where the certified baseline requires it.
