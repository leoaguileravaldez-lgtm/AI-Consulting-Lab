# Dissent and Response Model

## Immutable Chain

`ORIGINAL RECOMMENDATION -> CHALLENGE -> PRODUCER RESPONSE -> REVISED RECOMMENDATION -> RECHALLENGE -> UNRESOLVED DISSENT -> SYNTHESIS -> HUMAN PRINCIPAL DISPOSITION`

Every link binds exact object versions. Producer and challenger records are separately authored and sealed. Neither may edit, delete, downgrade, overwrite or withdraw the other's record.

## Producer Responses

Permitted subordinate response values are `ACCEPT`, `PARTIALLY_ACCEPT`, `REBUT`, `REVISE`, `REQUEST_ADDITIONAL_EVIDENCE`, and `UNRESOLVED`. They are not canonical states, decisions or blocker dispositions.

The response identifies challenge version, reasoning, evidence, assumptions, affected recommendation version, confidence effect, accepted/rebutted findings, unresolved matters and audit references. The producer may revise only its own recommendation through a new immutable version.

## Preservation Rules

Synthesis may reconcile positions but must preserve original recommendation, challenge, response, revision, unresolved dissent, residual uncertainty, confidence/limitations and Human Principal disposition. Majority vote, confidence averaging, seniority, source count, formatting, commercial preference or narrative compression cannot manufacture consensus.

Unresolved Material dissent must appear in the Material decision packet. Human Principal preference may determine action but cannot edit analytical history, change evidence quality, alter confidence or relabel unresolved dissent as resolved.

Commercial/client pressure and attempted suppression are recorded as conflict metadata. Challenge findings remain unchanged.
