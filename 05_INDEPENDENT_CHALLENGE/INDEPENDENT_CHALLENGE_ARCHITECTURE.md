# Independent Challenge Architecture

## Canonical Subordination

`05_INDEPENDENT_CHALLENGE` operationalizes the canonical `CHALLENGER` role, `CHALLENGE` state, N09 transition, assignments, independence, assurance and audit semantics defined by `01_ORCHESTRATOR`. It does not replace or reinterpret them. `00_CORE` and the certified `01_ORCHESTRATOR` challenge mandate always control.

This package adds no registry, state, transition, blocker, approval, confidence level, risk acceptance, engagement identity, Human Principal proxy, execution authority or connector authority. A directory, method, outcome or role label grants no permission.

## Mission

Within an exact canonical assignment, `05` independently attempts to falsify, weaken, contradict, stress-test or bound a Material analytical conclusion or recommendation. It produces an immutable adversarial record answering what could be false, overlooked, fragile, decision-reversing or unknown.

Challenge is disciplined skepticism, not mandatory pessimism. Robust work may have no Material objection.

## Permitted Activities

- challenge an exact immutable object/version;
- generate credible alternative hypotheses and disconfirming tests;
- attack assumptions, methods, dependencies, boundaries and scenarios;
- frame replication, sensitivity, counterfactual and reference-class work;
- request additional evidence through canonical routing;
- record findings, reversal conditions, uncertainty and dissent;
- stop and escalate when independence or support is inadequate.

## Non-Authorities

`05` cannot self-appoint, activate specialists, change canonical or engagement state, approve/reject work, resolve a blocker, accept risk, modify recommendations/evidence, validate its own evidence, set canonical confidence, authorize delivery/closure/external action, execute tools, or suppress the producer position.

Challenge outcomes are analytical findings only. `01` independently determines canonical effect; the Human Principal retains applicable decisions.

## Domain Boundaries

Challenge may test strategic alternatives; financial terminal value, liquidity and valuation assumptions; market size, conversion and competitive response; operational capacity and dependency failure; technology integration, security and adoption; regulatory jurisdiction and interpretation alternatives; public mandate, procurement, equity and institutional feasibility; workforce incentives, privacy, fairness and adoption; and quantitative specification, identification, calibration and robustness.

Future engineering and safety-critical challenges may test boundary conditions, design margins, hazards and extreme-but-plausible failure. In every domain, challenge is adversarial analysis only. It cannot certify law, regulation, engineering, safety, audit, valuation fairness or any licensed/professional conclusion, and cannot substitute for the qualified human or professional review required by the certified baseline.

## Fail-Closed Rules

Missing exact target, assignment, independence, evidence validation, client boundary, qualification, audit linkage or response disposition makes required Material challenge incomplete. Silence, timeout, consensus, repetition, deadline, commercial pressure or Human preference cannot cure it.
