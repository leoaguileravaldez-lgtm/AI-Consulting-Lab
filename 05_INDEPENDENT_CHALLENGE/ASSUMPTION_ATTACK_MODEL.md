# Assumption Attack Model

## Assumption Interface

Consume the certified assumption records and preserve:

- assumption ID/version and exact statement;
- owner and origin;
- explicit or detected-hidden status;
- materiality and decision-criticality;
- evidence support, validation and freshness;
- confidence, range and expiry;
- dependent claims, models, outputs and recommendations;
- sensitivity and decision-switch threshold;
- challenge, response and re-challenge status;
- audit linkage.

## Hidden-Assumption Detection

Inspect formulas, defaults, exclusions, scenarios, narrative transitions, causal language, dependency contracts, implementation plans and handoffs for propositions treated as true without a registered assumption or verified fact.

A Material conclusion dependent on an unregistered assumption is not silently decision-ready. The challenger records the assumption, affected dependencies and required evidence/remediation; `01` determines canonical effect.

## Attack Procedure

1. Rank assumptions by decision sensitivity, evidence weakness and dependency breadth.
2. Test plausible ranges, interactions and correlated failure.
3. Identify break-even and reversal values.
4. Seek disconfirming evidence through `04`.
5. Separate externally mandated facts from inherited producer beliefs.
6. Record robust, fragile, unsupported and unknown conditions.

An assumption cannot become fact through reuse, specialist agreement, synthesis, challenge response or Human approval.
