# Pre-Mortem and Failure Analysis

## Protocol

Assume the exact recommendation/version was implemented and materially failed. Ask:

- what mechanism caused failure;
- what signal existed beforehand;
- which assumption, dependency or causal claim failed;
- which stakeholder, competitor, regulator or institution responded differently;
- which operational, technology, workforce or market constraint was missed;
- which risk was underestimated;
- what prevention, mitigation or reversal condition existed.

Each failure hypothesis records plausibility, evidence, assumptions, early indicators, affected outcomes, prevention/mitigation ideas, residual uncertainty and audit linkage.

Failure hypotheses are ranked by decision relevance and credibility, not dramatic effect alone. Known incidents and base rates pass through `04` evidence controls.

Pre-mortem output is analytical. It cannot authorize implementation, corrective action, spending, deployment, risk acceptance or external communication. Future Risk/QA separately evaluates process controls and residual risk.
