# Challenge Independence

## Canonical Assignment

Only `01_ORCHESTRATOR` may assign the canonical `CHALLENGER`, create the challenge task/handoff, evaluate eligibility and commit state. The producer, researcher, validator and challenger cannot self-assign, substitute an actor or waive independence.

## Mechanical Dimensions

Before Material/Critical challenge begins, record:

- distinct actor identity from every target producer;
- model/provider/version and separate session identity;
- approved context manifest and access sequence;
- independently sealed assumption inventory;
- independent source-selection and contrary-evidence plan;
- genuinely adversarial method rather than restatement;
- prior participation/authorship check;
- financial, commercial, client, hierarchy and recommendation incentives;
- reporting path outside producer control;
- current subject, method, jurisdiction and data qualification;
- engagement, scope, permission and conflict eligibility.

A renamed role, different directory or actor label does not establish independence. Same producer/challenger session always fails. Shared hidden context, copied reasoning, identical untested assumptions, curated evidence only, primary-authored challenge text or producer-controlled reporting fails or limits independence under the certified matrix.

## Staged Context Protocol

1. `01` supplies decision question, exact target metadata, approved evidence manifest, inputs and acceptance criteria without persuasive producer narrative.
2. Challenger independently seals assumptions, alternative hypotheses, failure conditions, methods and research needs.
3. Challenger receives the exact challenged object and producer rationale.
4. Challenger performs and seals findings.
5. Producer receives the sealed challenge and may respond.

Producer-selected evidence alone is insufficient. The challenger must independently search for contrary evidence through `04` or document why an authorized search cannot be completed.

## Failure

Unavailable, conflicted, same-session, inadequately qualified, context-correlated or otherwise non-independent challenge fails closed. `05` publishes the independence failure; `01` determines the applicable canonical `BLOCKED_VALIDATION`, capacity, delegation or permission path. No timeout, majority view or Human preference creates independence.
