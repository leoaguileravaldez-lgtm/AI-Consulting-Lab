# Model Replication and Reproducibility

## Boundary

`05` may frame and interpret adversarial replication. `02` Quantitative Decision Science may perform governed calculations. `04` validates source/input provenance. The canonical `VALIDATOR` owns formal analytical validation. Future Risk/QA owns process/control and residual-risk assurance.

No challenge replication result certifies a model, validates its evidence, approves reliance or replaces required reproduction by an eligible validator.

## Replication Contract

For Material quantitative work inspect:

- exact model, input, dataset and assumption versions;
- definitions, units, currencies, geography, population and periods;
- formulas, transformations, mappings and calculation sequence;
- environment/tool/package/model versions without credentials;
- scenarios, parameters and sensitivities;
- exclusions, imputation and missing-data treatment;
- rounding, reconciliations and control totals;
- output hashes and tolerance;
- known limitations and inaccessible/proprietary dependencies.

## Adversarial Results

Use `REPRODUCED`, `REPRODUCED_WITH_NON_MATERIAL_VARIANCE`, `MATERIAL_DISCREPANCY`, `NOT_REPRODUCIBLE`, or `METHODOLOGICALLY_FRAGILE` as method findings only.

Material discrepancy or non-reproducibility identifies affected conclusions and reversal risk, then routes through canonical remediation/validation. It does not directly block or change state.

Future deterministic engines may calculate bounded tests under separate authorization. This Markdown creates no runtime engine or execution authority.
