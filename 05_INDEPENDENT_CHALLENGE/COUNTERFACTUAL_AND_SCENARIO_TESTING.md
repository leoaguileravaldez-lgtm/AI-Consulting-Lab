# Counterfactual and Scenario Testing

## Labeling

Counterfactuals and scenarios remain conditional analytical artifacts. They never become observed facts, forecasts, approvals or evidence through repetition.

Each test records:

- target object/version;
- changed condition and unchanged conditions;
- mechanism or causal rationale;
- horizon and jurisdiction;
- evidence and assumptions;
- probability status only where defensible;
- method/calculation owner;
- outputs and decision effect;
- reversal condition, confidence and limitations;
- audit reference.

## Required Tests

As applicable test lower demand, higher CAPEX/OPEX, delayed regulation, competitor response, doubled implementation time, failed integration, absent causal driver, workforce resistance, supplier failure, public-institution constraints and correlated downside.

Scenarios must be plausible and decision-relevant. Extreme-but-plausible tests state why their conditions are credible; deliberately impossible cases do not satisfy challenge.

`05` frames and interprets. `02` may compute. `04` validates supporting evidence. Future Risk/QA evaluates risk/control implications. No scenario authorizes mitigation or progression.
