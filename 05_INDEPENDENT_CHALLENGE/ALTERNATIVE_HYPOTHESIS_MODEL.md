# Alternative Hypothesis Model

## Standard

Every Material challenge constructs the strongest credible competing explanations that could materially change interpretation or action. Absurd or deliberately weak alternatives do not satisfy challenge.

Each alternative records hypothesis ID, mechanism, evidence for/against, assumptions, lineage, plausibility rationale, differentiating observations, test/research need, affected target, decision impact, confidence, limitations and audit reference.

## Procedure

1. State the producer hypothesis without distortion.
2. Generate alternatives before reviewing the producer response.
3. Test temporary, nominal, geographic, regulatory, supply, competitive, behavioral, measurement and data-artifact explanations as relevant.
4. Define evidence that distinguishes alternatives.
5. Route new research through `04` under `01` control.
6. Preserve credible unresolved alternatives in dissent and decision packets.

Example: apparent demand growth may reflect structural growth, a project cycle, inflation, geographic substitution, regulatory stimulus, shortage effects or a measurement artifact.

Source count, popularity and rhetorical plausibility do not determine the winning hypothesis. Evidence fitness, method and uncertainty remain explicit.
