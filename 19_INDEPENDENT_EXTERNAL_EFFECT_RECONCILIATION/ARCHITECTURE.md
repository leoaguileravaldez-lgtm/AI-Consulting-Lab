# Layer 19 — Independent External-Effect Reconciliation

## Purpose

Layer 19 owns one decision: the authoritative, independently evidenced determination of external-effect truth for an exact Layer 18 execution attempt and Layer 14 effect slot. It establishes effect presence, completeness, and cardinality for an exact target and target generation. It does not establish business outcome or close any lifecycle.

The architecture preserves these non-equivalences:

- execution evidence is not external-effect truth;
- external-effect truth is not business outcome;
- business outcome is not lifecycle closure;
- reconciliation verdict is neither execution nor retry authorization;
- reconciliation finding is not compensation authorization; and
- Human Principal approval is not external-effect evidence.

## Bounded authority

Layer 19 binds a predecessor-authorized reconciliation input, defines complete observation requirements, validates independent observer/reconciler assignment, admits external observations, assesses provenance and currentness, compares authoritative before/after state, and issues a dimensional reconciliation verdict. It may issue non-authorizing retry-prerequisite evidence, non-authorizing remediation/compensation-need findings, mandatory escalation requirements, invalidations, and bounded handoffs.

Layer 19 cannot originate or expand action authority; execute or retry an action; authorize compensation or remediation; mutate a target; evaluate business success; accept risk; close an action, engagement, recovery, or institutional lifecycle; replace Human authority; or acquire deployment/runtime-enforcement authority.

## Independent truth

The executor, adapter, action authorizer, and effective aliases cannot serve as observer or reconciler for the same Material scope. The reconciler cannot be the sole Material observation producer, outcome evaluator, or closure owner. Executor evidence and adapter acknowledgements are descendant context only. Observation membership, provenance, limitations, missing data, stale data, and contradictions are immutable inputs to reconciliation and cannot be favorably selected or voted away.

## Dimensional verdict

Effect presence, completeness, cardinality, reconciliation process, observation status, and lifecycle status are independent dimensions constrained by the canonical consistency rules. Confirmed no-effect requires current complete evidence, completed reconciliation, zero cardinality, and not-applicable completeness. Missing, stale, incomplete, failed, ambiguous, or conflicting evidence never implies no effect.

Partial and duplicate effects remain distinguishable. A partial duplicated effect preserves both facts. Contradictory histories remain contradictory. Generation changes, revocations, material evidence changes, expiry, and supersession invalidate dependent verdicts, retry prerequisites, and downstream reliance.

## Retry, compensation, Human, and closure boundaries

A current `NO_EFFECT_CONFIRMED` verdict may satisfy a non-authorizing retry prerequisite, but any new execution still requires the complete certified predecessor authorization chain, a new Layer 18 attempt, and a Layer 14 protected-effect barrier. Unknown, partial, duplicate, contradictory, failed, or stale states fail closed.

A Layer 19 finding may require remediation, compensation, incident, or Human review. It cannot decide or execute them. Any compensating external action is a new ordinary authorized action. Human authority may determine governance responses and accept defined residual risk where predecessors allow, but cannot manufacture external truth.

## Phase transition

After Layer 19 reaches independent architectural certification, controlled implementation of certified contracts is presumptively next. No Layer 20 or later abstract responsibility is authorized or justified here. Additional layering requires new empirical evidence of a genuine gap or a newly demonstrated material authority responsibility.
