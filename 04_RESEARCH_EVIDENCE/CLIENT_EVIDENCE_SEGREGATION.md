# Client Evidence Segregation

## Evidence Classes

- `PUBLIC_REUSABLE_EVIDENCE`: lawfully accessible non-client source, reusable only after engagement-specific freshness, applicability, jurisdiction and provenance checks.
- `REUSABLE_METHODOLOGY`: blank methods, schemas and sanitized procedures governed by the future Knowledge module; contains no client facts.
- `CLIENT_SPECIFIC_EVIDENCE`: client-originated or engagement-specific facts restricted to the engagement.
- `CLIENT_CONFIDENTIAL_EVIDENCE`: client-specific evidence subject to heightened contractual/security restrictions.

Similarity never authorizes factual reuse. De-identification alone does not turn client evidence into public evidence or generic knowledge.

## Structural Controls

Every evidence, source, retrieval, claim relation, snapshot, index, cache, embedding, context manifest, contradiction and impact record carries immutable engagement ID, client/security domain, data classification, purpose, access policy, retention and audit linkage.

Cross-boundary access defaults to deny. Physical/logical storage namespaces, identity groups and encryption/key boundaries apply where required by certified security controls. A source link cannot bypass content authorization.

## Negative Controls

- Client A record retrieved for Client B: deny, quarantine result, audit and invoke applicable security/incident handling.
- Copied workstream/source ID with foreign engagement lineage: reject mismatch.
- Client evidence in shared cache, prompt memory, benchmark or embedding: suppress and quarantine.
- Similar client, sector or question: perform new applicability research; inherit no factual support.
- Public source previously used for another client: reuse source identity only if authorized; create engagement-bound retrieval, applicability and claim relations.
- Reusable method containing client residue: reject sanitation and sharing.

Specialist memory, summaries and prior deliverables are not evidence retrieval mechanisms. Unknown or mixed lineage fails closed.

## Future Knowledge Boundary

Only the future Knowledge module may govern reusable knowledge promotion. `04` may propose a public-source or sanitized-method candidate but cannot publish, share or reclassify client content autonomously.
