# Source Freshness and Versioning

## Freshness Status

Use `CURRENT`, `AGING`, `STALE`, `SUPERSEDED`, `REVOKED`, or `UNKNOWN`. These are evidence/source statuses, not canonical task states.

Freshness evaluates reporting period, publication date, retrieval time, source-change rate, authoritative supersession check, claim horizon and delivery/reliance time. Retrieval today does not make old content current.

Canonical minimums remain controlling: changeable facts are checked against an authoritative current source within 24 hours before external delivery/action; the latest appropriate company/government release is rechecked within 30 days; other sources older than 12 months require continued-relevance rationale unless historical or slowly changing.

| Claim | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Price, rate, sanction, current official or executive position | Authoritative current check, normally within 24 hours |
| Regulation/guidance | Current official text, jurisdiction and effective/as-of date |
| Market share | Latest appropriate period, method/definition match and release check |
| Demographics | Latest appropriate official release; measurement and publication dates distinct |
| Engineering standard | Current edition, amendment and applicable adoption check |
| Historical financial statement | Preserve period; check restatement/supersession status |
| Historical research | Limit claim to historical scope or document continued relevance |

## Immutable Versioning

Every version records source identity/version, content fingerprint or authorized snapshot reference, retrieval proof, status, changed fields, correction/revocation/supersession links, reason, affected relations and audit event. Corrections append a new version and preserve the original.

A vanished URL does not erase a lawful verified snapshot, but accessibility, authenticity and continued fitness must be reassessed. Without adequate preservation or retrieval proof, the evidence cannot continue supporting a Material claim by memory or citation metadata alone.

## Change Effect

Correction, revocation, expiry, supersession, provenance failure, material reinterpretation or freshness failure immediately suspends affected reliance and initiates the propagation contract. A prior Human Principal approval cannot preserve invalid evidence support.
