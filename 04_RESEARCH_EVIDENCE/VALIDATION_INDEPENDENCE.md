# Validation Independence

## Role Separation

| Role | Permitted function | Non-authority |
|---|---|---|
| Researcher | Discover, retrieve and document authorized sources | Cannot solely validate its selected Material evidence |
| Primary analyst | Interpret evidence within domain analysis | Cannot select, interpret and validate the sole Material basis |
| Evidence validator | Independently retrieve and test source/claim support | Cannot be primary preparer or approve reliance |
| Independent challenger | Future independent falsification of analysis/recommendation | Not owned by `04` |

For Material evidence or conclusions, no actor may solely select the evidence, interpret it, validate it and certify reliance. No actor can certify reliance at all; canonical reliance and Human decisions remain outside `04`.

## Mechanical Independence

Before validation, `01_ORCHESTRATOR` must assign an eligible validator and the canonical independence record must pass actor identity, separate session/context lineage, qualification, conflicts, independent retrieval, sealed first-pass assumption inventory, method, and evidence provenance checks.

The initial validation packet excludes persuasive recommendation narrative. The validator independently retrieves decision-critical sources, searches for contrary evidence, verifies exact support coordinates, tests lineage/corroboration and seals first-pass findings before comparison with the primary analysis.

Different labels, multiple specialists, the same actor in a new role, shared hidden context, primary-supplied summaries or cached citations cannot establish independence. Same-model/provider limitations follow the certified correlation matrix. Critical work requires qualified human review.

## Failure Behavior

Unavailable, conflicted, unqualified, same-session, circular or otherwise inadequate validation produces `FAIL` or permitted `LIMITED`, an evidence-impact notice, confidence treatment under `00_CORE`, and notification for canonical `BLOCKED_VALIDATION`. Material/Critical reliance stops. `04` cannot appoint a substitute, waive independence or approve an exception.

Researcher self-check is encouraged for accuracy but is never formal independent validation.
