# Source Quality Model

## Dimensional Assessment

Source fitness follows `00_CORE/source_validation/SOURCE_VALIDATION.md`. Assess every relevant dimension as `PASS`, `LIMITED`, `FAIL`, or `UNKNOWN` with rationale:

- authority and accountable scope;
- proximity/directness to original evidence;
- identity and provenance integrity;
- methodology transparency and fitness;
- reproducibility;
- completeness and missing-data treatment;
- temporal relevance and supersession;
- jurisdiction, population, definition and unit relevance;
- independence, sponsorship and conflicts;
- publication/editorial controls;
- consistency and credible contradictions;
- known limitations and alteration risk.

`UNKNOWN` is not neutral. A decision-critical `FAIL` cannot be averaged away. A category or institutional reputation cannot override a failed applicability, freshness, provenance or method dimension.

## Summary Band

A transparent summary may be `STRONG`, `ADEQUATE_WITH_LIMITATIONS`, `WEAK`, or `UNUSABLE`. The full dimension vector remains visible. The band does not determine canonical claim confidence, validation, corroboration, approval or reliance.

## Hard Stops

Material support fails when source identity is unconfirmed, content is inaccessible or mismatched, provenance is fabricated, the applicable jurisdiction fails, decision-critical methodology is unusable, a current claim relies on revoked/superseded evidence, or required retrieval proof is absent.

Opaque methodology lowers fitness and cannot independently verify a decision-critical Material claim. Authority bias is prohibited: an authoritative issuer can still be irrelevant, outdated, conflicted, incomplete or methodologically unfit.

## Bias Controls

The research record includes selection protocol, inclusion/exclusion rationale, contrary-source search, sponsor and incentive review, missing-source log, and unresolved limitations. Cherry-picking or client-pressure selection invalidates completeness and requires independent review.
