# Evidence Contradiction Model

## Record

Every contradiction records competing claims and evidence, definitions, periods, populations, jurisdictions, units, source-quality dimensions, provenance/lineage, materiality, severity, resolution attempts, unresolved uncertainty, dependency impact, confidence adjustment, owner, assurance references, version and audit linkage.

Severity is `ROUTINE`, `MATERIAL`, or `CRITICAL` by controlling materiality; it is not a substitute state. The canonical contradiction dispositions remain `RESOLVED`, `ACCEPTED_NON_MATERIAL_LIMITATION`, or `UNRESOLVED`.

## Preservation

No researcher, specialist, validator, synthesizer or deliverable function may delete, average, summarize out, rhetorically soften or select away credible contradiction. Both positions remain linked through evidence, analysis, recommendation, decision packet and revalidation.

An unresolved Material contradiction:

- remains nonterminal and visible;
- caps affected confidence under the certified rules;
- triggers an evidence-impact notice and canonical `BLOCKED_SOURCE_CONFLICT` consideration;
- blocks release when it could change recommendation, reliance or risk acceptance;
- requires Human Principal visibility.

Human Principal authority may select among transparently presented alternatives or accept a permitted non-Material limitation. It cannot convert false support into validated evidence or silently resolve a Material evidentiary conflict.

## Resolution Method

Compare source identity, definitions, dates, jurisdictions, population, method, incentives, lineage, transformations and applicability. Resolution must cite the new evidence or reasoning and identify all downstream effects. Mere source prestige, majority count or narrative convenience is invalid.

Independent Challenge remains outside `04`; it may add contradictions or challenge dispositions but does not transfer its mandate to Research/Evidence.
