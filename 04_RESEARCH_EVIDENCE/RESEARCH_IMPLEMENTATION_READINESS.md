# Research and Evidence Implementation Readiness

## Design-Only Boundary

This package is non-executable Markdown. It authorizes no research runtime, browsing, connector, credential, purchase, subscription, message, email, upload, client access or external action.

## Mandatory Blocker Resolution

1. Canonical policy/schema/state/confidence authority remains in `00_CORE` and `01_ORCHESTRATOR`; `04` operationalizes only.
2. Research labels deterministically map to existing canonical claim types; illegal conversions fail.
3. Material research selection and validation require mechanical separation and canonical assignment.
4. `04` issues evidence-impact notices; `01` alone owns blockers/transitions and `03` reflects readiness.
5. Source taxonomy separates type, provenance, authority, quality, independence, freshness, jurisdiction and method.
6. Tool fields are descriptive and confer no runtime or external authority.
7. Fabrication, access failure, staleness, revocation, supersession, lineage dependence, contradiction, bias, self-validation, contamination, jurisdiction mismatch and unsupported AI citations fail closed.

Ambiguity resolves to the more restrictive certified rule and blocks implementation.
