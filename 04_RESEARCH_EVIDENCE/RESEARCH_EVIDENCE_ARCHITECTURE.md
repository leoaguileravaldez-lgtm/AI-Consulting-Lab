# Research and Evidence Architecture

## Canonical Subordination

`04_RESEARCH_EVIDENCE` operationalizes bounded research, source identity, retrieval proof, provenance, lineage, evidence assessment, freshness, contradiction preservation, and evidence-impact notices. It does not replace or reinterpret `00_CORE/source_validation/SOURCE_VALIDATION.md`, `00_CORE/quality_standards/QUALITY_STANDARDS.md`, or the canonical claims, evidence, confidence, assurance, schemas, states, blockers, roles, audit, and approval controls in `01_ORCHESTRATOR`.

If this package differs from a certified rule, the certified rule controls and the affected work fails canonical-drift review. `04` adds no task state, transition, approval, confidence level, permission, registry, engagement identity, risk acceptance, external action, or Human Principal proxy.

## Mission

Within an authorized engagement and canonical task, `04` may:

- receive a bounded research request routed by `01_ORCHESTRATOR`;
- acquire already-authorized evidence through future governed interfaces;
- create immutable source, retrieval, evidence, lineage, relation, contradiction, and impact records;
- evaluate source fitness dimensionally and identify correlated roots;
- assign subordinate evidence-assessment results;
- independently validate evidence when separately assigned and eligible;
- notify `01` when evidence support, freshness, provenance, or applicability changes.

It may not choose strategy, redefine a decision, activate specialists, lower materiality, approve an engagement or reliance, certify a recommendation, accept risk, authorize expenditure/delivery/closure, change canonical state, contact an external party, or execute a connector autonomously.

## Fail-Closed Invariants

- Unknown identity, access, provenance, lineage, freshness, jurisdiction, applicability or validation cannot be treated as satisfied.
- Citation count never substitutes for independent lineage.
- A researcher, analyst, or module cannot self-assign or self-certify Material support.
- A source record is not proof that a source was accessed or that it supports a claim.
- A subordinate `SUPPORTED` result is not approval, High confidence, or authorization.
- Material uncertainty exceeding validated support produces an impact notice and canonical block/revalidation request.
- Human Principal approval cannot make false, stale, revoked, unsupported or contaminated evidence valid.

## Audit Minimum

Every Material research event records engagement/task, actor/session/role assignment, request, source identity, access outcome, content coordinates, versions/hashes where lawful, provenance, transformations, quality dimensions, lineage groups, claim relations, validation, contradictions, impact notices, timestamps and audit linkage. Corrections append; history is never overwritten.
