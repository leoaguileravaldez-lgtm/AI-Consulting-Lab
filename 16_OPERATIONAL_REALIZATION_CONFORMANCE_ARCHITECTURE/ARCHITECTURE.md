## Responsibility

Layer 16 defines the conformance contract by which the certified semantics of Layers 00–15 may eventually be represented, persisted, protected, coordinated, recovered, isolated, observed and empirically tested. It specifies required properties and evidence; it does not choose, implement, operate, deploy, release or certify a production mechanism.

The canonical contract path is:

`CERTIFIED LAYERS 00–15 BASELINE → REALIZATION SCOPE → EXACT REQUIREMENT BINDINGS → REPRESENTATION → IDENTITY/VERSION/PROVENANCE → INTEGRITY + PERSISTENCE → UNIQUENESS/CONCURRENCY → REVOCATION/FRESHNESS → ISOLATION/ATTESTATION → RECOVERY → TELEMETRY/AUDIT → FUTURE EMPIRICAL CERTIFICATION CONTRACT → DEFERRED IMPLEMENTATION ALLOCATION → RECONCILED CONFORMANCE SPECIFICATION`.

## Authority boundary

Layer 16 owns only realization-conformance requirements, exact predecessor mappings, future implementation evidence contracts, future empirical test obligations and architectural fail-closed eligibility criteria. It creates no business, Human, evidence, analysis, decision, access, release, deployment, recovery, delivery or external-action authority.

Layer 14 remains the sole formal owner of protected-action runtime-enforcement semantics. Layer 16 binds realization requirements to Layer 14 typed events, closures, projections, global effect slots, barriers, revocation and recovery epochs; it creates no second runtime model.

Layer 15 remains the owner of consulting engagement orchestration, evidence-to-conclusion lineage and non-executing handoffs. Serialization, deployment, adapters and technical capability cannot convert a Layer 15 handoff into an executable instruction.

Deployment and operational assurance governance, and external-action execution with effect/outcome reconciliation, remain materially distinct future responsibilities. This package assigns neither a layer number.

## Conformance semantics

Every applicable Material predecessor requirement has an exact binding to one or more Layer 16 obligations. Representation is deterministic and typed. Realization identity binds exact semantic identity, version/hash, authority owner, boundary, provenance, lifecycle, freshness and revocation. Physical existence or technical success never creates semantic authority.

Authoritative state and required audit evidence commit under an explicit atomicity rule. Exclusive successors, consumptions and effects use certified global uniqueness domains; process-local idempotency is insufficient. Stale writers and ambiguous partition outcomes fail closed. Revocation dominates caches, queues, sessions, retries and replicas.

Recovery preserves history, consumed authority and effect uniqueness. Unknown or partial outcomes reconcile before retry. Runtime attestation proves only the bounded workload facts it is qualified to prove and cannot create identity or authority. Telemetry and audit remain descendant evidence and cannot repair a missing predecessor.

Future empirical certification binds an exact candidate, environment and dependency manifest to finite Material attack families, historical counterexamples, expected outcomes, independent validator qualifications and retest triggers. Architecture-only validation never certifies an operational implementation.
