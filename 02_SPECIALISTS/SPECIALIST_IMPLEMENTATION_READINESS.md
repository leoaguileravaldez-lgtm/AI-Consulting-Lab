# Specialist Implementation Readiness

## Status

This package is architecture and design only. It authorizes no runtime, agent, tool, credential, external access, client processing, state mutation, deployment, delivery, commit, or execution.

## Preconditions for Future Implementation Planning

- Human Principal approval of the exact design baseline;
- complete traceability to certified `00_CORE` and `01_ORCHESTRATOR` v1.1;
- no canonical drift in states, roles, tiers, approval categories, materiality, confidence, evidence, or security controls;
- enforceable typed registry identifiers and role/permission separation;
- validated specialist-selection and anti-duplication logic;
- mechanically enforceable handoff, engagement binding, and dependency invalidation;
- independent challenge and validation assignment controls;
- reproducibility controls for Material quantitative work;
- qualified human review routing for Critical and regulated-profession work;
- trusted approval, audit, registry, identity, permission, and security boundaries outside specialist control;
- fail-closed error, capacity, freshness, and recovery behavior;
- conformance tests and independent negative testing;
- approved operational parameters, capacity limits, and recovery objectives.

## Required Conformance Families

Future implementation must test:

1. routing selects the minimum eligible team and rejects unnecessary or ineligible activation;
2. module, practice, capability, role, actor, and session identifiers cannot substitute for one another;
3. practice membership or filesystem location grants no authority;
4. same actor/session cannot self-challenge or self-validate Material work;
5. shared source lineage is not counted as independent corroboration;
6. unvalidated inputs remain provisional through downstream handoffs;
7. Material model reproduction failure blocks reliance;
8. contradiction records cannot be omitted or averaged away;
9. Human Principal unavailability produces no progression;
10. specialist output cannot trigger external action;
11. cross-engagement data, cache, prompt, or audit references are denied;
12. credential, permission, approval, audit, and security boundary failures open the applicable breaker;
13. no specialist can lower materiality, clear its conflict, activate its registry entry, or accept its own risk;
14. deliverable support cannot change validated meaning;
15. state-machine and approval semantics remain owned by `01_ORCHESTRATOR`.
16. invented KPIs and Material metrics without accountable domain ownership are rejected;
17. approved baselines cannot be overwritten or retrospectively manipulated;
18. selective reporting, metric substitution, cherry-picked windows, and suppression of failed outcomes are detected and blocked;
19. unsupported attribution and correlation presented as causation fail validation;
20. overlapping or transferred benefits cannot be double counted;
21. the metric designer, benefit owner, implementation owner, and quantitative producer cannot solely validate affected Material results;
22. unfavorable results and unintended consequences remain visible through synthesis and Deliverables;
23. metric results and corrective recommendations cannot autonomously authorize action or stage progression.

## Adversarial Scenario Requirements

| Scenario | Required containment |
|---|---|
| Founder-thesis bias | Alternative hypotheses, disconfirming evidence, independent challenge, visible reversal conditions |
| Finance uses unvalidated assumptions | Provisional labeling; block final feasibility; return to evidence owner |
| Legal/regulatory uncertainty | Stop and route qualified human review; no AI legal-advice representation |
| Operational infeasibility | Preserve contradiction; invalidate strategy feasibility; return for redesign |
| Common-source failure | Count lineage once; independently retrieve; invalidate dependent claims if flawed |
| Unnecessary activation | Reject without bounded marginal-value rationale |
| Public-sector misclassification | Raise routing/classification; require public mandate, authority, procurement, equity, and institutional review |
| Technology over-weighting | Require commercial, financial, operational, and workforce evidence where decision-critical |
| Specialist conflict | Open contradiction/conflict record; assign neutral resolution; preserve dissent |
| Human Principal unavailable | Hold or authorization/capacity block; never infer approval |
| Unresolved contradiction before final recommendation | Block release when recommendation- or risk-changing; disclose to Human Principal |
| Output crosses into external action | Deny action; require exact Tier 4 approval and authorized executor outside specialist authority |
| Retroactive baseline manipulation | Preserve approved version; open impact review; require revalidation and renewed approval where Material |
| Selective KPI reporting | Require all approved Material KPIs, missed targets, disbenefits, and unintended-consequence measures |
| Unsupported benefit attribution | Downgrade to contribution, association, or no defensible attribution; block causal benefit claim |
| Double-counted benefits | Reconcile unique benefit IDs, owners, dependencies, overlap, transfer, and cannibalization |
| Unfavorable realized outcome | Preserve result, confidence, and dissent; prohibit alteration or suppression in synthesis/Deliverables |
| Corrective recommendation treated as action | Deny progression or execution; require applicable Human Principal decision and exact authorization |
