# Specialist Conflict and Dependency Model

## Objective

This model prevents conflicts of interest, correlated reasoning, circular assumptions, and hidden dependency failure across specialist work.

## Conflict Types

- engagement, client, competitor, prior-client, personal, financial, vendor, or incentive conflict;
- authorship or review-role conflict;
- professional or jurisdictional qualification conflict;
- data-use, confidentiality, privilege, consent, or purpose conflict;
- recommendation ownership that could bias validation or risk review;
- apparent conflict that could reasonably undermine trust even without proven bias.

Actual or apparent unresolved conflict disqualifies the affected actor from challenge, validation, risk review, approval, or risk acceptance. Mitigation and consent follow `00_CORE`; a specialist cannot self-clear a conflict.

## Correlated-Reasoning Risks

Correlation includes:

- same actor, session, hidden context, or persuasive primary narrative;
- same model/provider, prompt framing, template, or cached output;
- common source lineage presented as corroboration;
- shared untested assumptions or management forecasts;
- shared code, formulas, datasets, priors, or benchmark leakage;
- hierarchical pressure from a lead practice or founder/client thesis;
- repeated secondary sources derived from one original dataset;
- deliverable framing that anchors reviewers to a preferred conclusion.

Different labels or directories do not establish independence. The certified independence record controls.

## Dependency Contract

Each dependency identifies provider, consumer, exact input/output version, required validation status, freshness deadline, assumption/claim types, materiality, confidence, acceptance criteria, invalidation triggers, and audit linkage.

Decision-critical dependencies must be stable and validated for the intended use before downstream reliance. Provisional inputs may be used only in explicitly labeled scenarios and cannot support an ordinary Material recommendation.

## Required Cross-Practice Constraints

- Finance may model unvalidated Market inputs only as labeled provisional scenarios; it may not report them as facts or issue final feasibility.
- Strategy may propose an option before Operations review but may not state operating feasibility as established.
- Technology feasibility cannot establish customer value, adoption, revenue, or financial return.
- Commercial projections must reconcile to Market definitions, Operations capacity, and Finance economics.
- Regulatory/Policy analysis may flag issues but cannot certify legal permissibility or compliance.
- Public Sector work must test public mandate, authority, procurement, equity, and institutional capacity rather than importing private-sector logic alone.
- Organization conclusions must disclose workforce populations, privacy, fairness, labor, and adoption limitations.
- Quantitative support cannot validate a model it produced.

## Invalidation

A Material change to evidence, assumptions, definitions, scope, jurisdiction, method, model, confidence, risk, recipient, or approval invalidates affected dependencies. The Orchestrator identifies every downstream consumer and returns work to the earliest affected canonical state. Superseded outputs remain in audit lineage and cannot be silently overwritten.

## Conflict Resolution

1. Freeze affected reliance.
2. Preserve both positions and their lineage.
3. Separate factual from judgmental disagreement.
4. Assign independent resolution work.
5. Seek disconfirming evidence and alternative methods.
6. Test downstream decision sensitivity.
7. Recalculate confidence and risk.
8. Resolve, accept only a non-Material limitation, or retain as unresolved.
9. Escalate recommendation-changing or risk-changing Material conflict.

The Orchestrator synthesizes disclosed positions; it cannot average incompatible conclusions or allow one practice to dominate by seniority, volume, or rhetorical confidence.

## Stop Conditions

Stop when conflict eligibility is unresolved, evidence lineage is shared but represented as independent, a dependency is stale or unvalidated, circular assumptions are detected, contradiction disposition is missing, or an upstream change has not been propagated.
