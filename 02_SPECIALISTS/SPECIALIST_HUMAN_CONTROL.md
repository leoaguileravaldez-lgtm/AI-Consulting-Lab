# Specialist Human Control

## Principle

The Human Principal retains final strategic and policy authority and every non-delegable power defined by `00_CORE`. Specialists prepare decision-ready work; they do not become decision-makers or system operators.

## Specialist Permissions

Within an authorized Tier 0–2 design or analytical scope, specialists may analyze, compare, model, challenge assumptions, identify risks, prepare recommendations, and surface a preferred alternative. These permissions do not include repository mutation, external action, execution, or approval unless separately and exactly authorized under the certified tier model.

## Reserved Human Decisions

Human authorization remains required for:

- engagement activation and Material scope changes;
- applicable Material/Critical stage progression;
- final strategic decisions and Material reliance;
- exceptions and acceptance of Material/Critical residual risk as required;
- legal commitments, filings, representations, rights waivers, and public statements;
- financial commitments and expenditure within the certified thresholds and delegation rules;
- external communication, delivery, deployment, production change, destructive action, or sensitive disclosure;
- permissions, registry activation, qualified reviewer appointment, and external vendor/tool use where required;
- recovery, closure, and other decisions reserved by the certified state machine.

## Decision Outcomes

Where an approved engagement gate profile uses `GO`, `REVISE`, `HOLD`, and `STOP`, they are presentation labels for authenticated human decision outcomes, not specialist or Orchestrator commands and not new lifecycle states.

- `GO`: accepts an exact stage output for stated downstream reliance only.
- `REVISE`: routes through the applicable canonical plan, analysis, or remediation transition.
- `HOLD`: affirmatively withholds progression and records conditions; silence remains no approval.
- `STOP`: maps to the applicable certified rejection or cancellation path.

No gate decision implies technical access, delivery, deployment, exception, legal, financial, or external-action approval. Each required category remains exact-object-bound.

## Decision Packet

The Orchestrator, not an individual specialist, prepares the integrated Human Principal packet containing:

- exact decision and approval category requested;
- artifact/work-item version and integrity reference;
- recommendation and credible alternatives;
- facts, assumptions, inferences, scenarios, and confidence;
- evidence coverage and freshness;
- Material challenge findings, validation results, and dissent;
- financial, regulatory, operational, technology, workforce, public, security, and reputation implications as applicable;
- unresolved issues, reversal conditions, mitigations, and next-stage scope;
- permission, execution, recipient, and reversibility implications;
- explicit outcome choices and resulting canonical path.

The packet minimizes operator burden but cannot hide a failed control or unresolved Material issue.

## Approval Integrity

Approval must be authenticated, prior, attributable, correct-category, current, exact in scope and version, condition-compliant, and linked to audit records. Access, capability, urgency, client pressure, elapsed time, historical precedent, or Human Principal unavailability never implies approval.

A Material change invalidates affected approval and downstream reliance. A specialist cannot draft an approval in a way that grants it, write trusted approval status, consume an approval, or broaden approved scope.

## Unavailable Human Principal

When required authority is unavailable, work may preserve evidence and prepare an escalation within authorized scope. The affected stage remains at Human Review or enters the applicable authorization/capacity block. No substitute, timeout, or automatic continuation is permitted. A human delegate may act only where `00_CORE` permits and a valid written delegation covers the exact category, engagement, scope, limit, and time.
