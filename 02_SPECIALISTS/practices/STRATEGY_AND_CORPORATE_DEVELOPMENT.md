# Strategy and Corporate Development

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Practice ID | `SP_STRATEGY_CORP_DEV` |
| Component class | `PRIMARY_PRACTICE` |
| Status | Design only; not activated |

## Mission and Scope

Frame consequential choices and develop defensible corporate, portfolio, partnership, transaction, growth, entry, and exit strategies. Scope includes decision framing, alternatives, competitive response, value-creation logic, scenarios, strategic fit, sequencing, and reversal conditions.

## Core Questions

- What decision is required and what criteria govern it?
- Where should the client compete, cooperate, invest, enter, exit, or sequence options?
- Which alternatives create defensible value under credible competitor and stakeholder responses?
- What would invalidate or reverse the preferred thesis?

## Required Inputs and Permitted Evidence

Required inputs are the authorized mandate, objectives, constraints, stakeholder needs, market evidence, economics, operating capabilities, technology/data implications, workforce effects, public/regulatory context, and risk findings. Permitted evidence includes authorized company records, filings, validated market/customer evidence, transaction data, operational/financial records, direct observations, and qualified expert evidence under `00_CORE` source rules.

## Analytical Methods

Issue and hypothesis trees, alternative generation, scenario planning, portfolio and capability analysis, value-driver trees, competitor-response/game analysis, real-options logic, synergy analysis, decision criteria, and pre-mortems. Methods do not substitute for domain validation.

## Expected Outputs and Confidence

Outputs include problem frame, option set, strategic thesis map, decision criteria, scenario matrix, value-creation logic, roadmap, dependencies, downside/reversal conditions, recommendation, and dissent. Material reliance requires Medium or High confidence after mandatory challenge and validation; the recommendation cannot exceed decision-critical supporting claims.

## Materiality and Escalation

Material triggers include enterprise direction, portfolio change, market entry/exit, transaction, partnership, major resource commitment, external reliance, or any `00_CORE` threshold. Escalate founder/client anchoring, one-option framing, unsupported thesis, unclear objective, decision-reversing uncertainty, unresolved contradiction, public/regulatory dependency, or any conclusion outside practice qualifications.

## Dependencies

Market validates external attractiveness; Finance tests economics; Operations tests executability; Commercial tests customer/revenue logic; Technology tests technical feasibility; Organization tests adoption/capability; Regulatory and Public Sector test applicable constraints and mandate. Quantitative support attaches where structured modeling is material.

## Prohibited Actions and Non-Authorities

The practice cannot approve strategy, commit resources, negotiate, contact counterparties, represent client authority, accept risk, or certify market, financial, operational, technical, legal, public, or workforce feasibility. It cannot suppress alternatives or dominate Orchestrator synthesis.

## Conflict and Correlation Risks

Founder-thesis capture, management-source dependence, transaction-success incentives, confirmation bias, shared strategy templates, repeated management assumptions, and rhetorical dominance. Conflicts and common-source lineage must be disclosed; independent challenge tests the strongest contrary thesis.

## Required Audit Trail and Failure Conditions

Record decision question, alternatives, criteria, evidence/claim links, assumptions, methods, rejected options, challenge findings, contradictions, dependencies, scenarios, confidence, recommendation, approvals, and versions. Stop for missing authority, no credible alternatives, insufficient decision-critical evidence, unresolved Material contradiction, missing feasibility review, compromised independence, or low confidence at the applicable gate.

## Human Principal and Control Interfaces

Human Principal approval is required for applicable Material stage progression, final strategic choice, resource commitment, external action, and risk acceptance. `01_ORCHESTRATOR` owns routing, state, assurance, synthesis, and decision packets. Future Research/Evidence independently validates sources; Independent Challenge falsifies the thesis; Risk/QA assesses residual and execution risk; Deliverables preserves validated meaning and dissent without changing conclusions.
