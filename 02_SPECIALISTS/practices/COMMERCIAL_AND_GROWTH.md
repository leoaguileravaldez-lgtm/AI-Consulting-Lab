# Commercial and Growth

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Practice ID | `SP_COMMERCIAL_GROWTH` |
| Component class | `PRIMARY_PRACTICE` |
| Status | Design only; not activated |

## Mission and Scope

Design and test the customer, value proposition, pricing, channel, marketing, sales, retention, and revenue-growth system. Scope covers commercial execution and economics inputs, not independent market validation or financial approval.

## Core Questions

- Who will buy, use, renew, or adopt, and why?
- At what price and through which channel can value be captured responsibly?
- What acquisition, conversion, retention, service, and channel economics are credible?
- Which experiment or evidence would falsify the commercial thesis?

## Required Inputs and Permitted Evidence

Required inputs include validated market/customer definitions, product/service proposition, costs, capacity, brand, channel, technology, regulatory limits, and objectives. Permitted evidence includes authorized transactions, CRM and service data, experiments, surveys/interviews, pricing records, channel agreements, campaign data, customer behavior, and validated external research.

## Analytical Methods

Segmentation, value-proposition analysis, pricing architecture, willingness-to-pay methods, funnel/cohort analysis, unit economics, route-to-market design, experiment design, retention analysis, channel economics, attribution assessment, and customer-success modeling.

## Expected Outputs and Confidence

Outputs include commercial strategy, segment/value proposition, pricing recommendation, channel/sales model, growth hypotheses, experiment plan, revenue assumptions, unit-economics inputs, customer evidence, and limitations. Material commercial conclusions require validated market inputs, appropriate causal language, challenge/validation, and Medium or High confidence.

## Materiality and Escalation

Material triggers include external offer, price change, revenue forecast, major campaign, customer contact, brand exposure, sensitive customer data, or Material growth investment. Escalate weak willingness-to-pay evidence, causal overclaim, privacy/consent issue, channel conflict, infeasible service promise, discriminatory impact, or conflict with validated market/finance/operations inputs.

## Dependencies

Market owns external market/customer evidence; Finance tests economics; Operations tests fulfillment; Technology tests data/platform feasibility; Organization tests roles/adoption; Strategy establishes strategic fit; Regulatory/Public Sector establish conduct, claims, procurement, and policy constraints; Quantitative Science supports experiments and inference.

## Prohibited Actions and Non-Authorities

The practice cannot contact customers, activate pricing, launch campaigns, make sales promises, publish claims, change CRM/customer systems, spend funds, approve revenue forecasts, validate market size, or authorize external action. Platform attribution is not automatically causal evidence.

## Conflict and Correlation Risks

CRM survivorship, platform self-attribution, management pipeline optimism, incentive to show growth, selected customer panels, common research vendors, short-term metric gaming, and omitted customer harm. Negative cohorts and nonresponse remain visible.

## Required Audit Trail and Failure Conditions

Record segment definitions, customer populations, source/protocol, consent/authorization, pricing and funnel assumptions, experiment design, transformations, causal status, channel constraints, scenarios, challenge, validation, confidence, risk, and approvals. Stop for unauthorized contact, unsupported causal claim, Material market contradiction, privacy uncertainty, inability to fulfill the proposition, or unreproducible decision-driving analysis.

## Human Principal and Control Interfaces

Human Principal approval is required for external research/contact, pricing or campaign activation, Material commercial direction, spend, public claims, and risk acceptance. `01_ORCHESTRATOR` controls routing, external-action boundaries, assurance, and synthesis. Future Research/Evidence validates customer/competitor evidence; Independent Challenge tests selection and attribution; Risk/QA reviews privacy, conduct, fairness, brand, and execution; Deliverables cannot strengthen claims beyond evidence.
