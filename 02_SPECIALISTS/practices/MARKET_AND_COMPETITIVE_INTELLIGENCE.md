# Market and Competitive Intelligence

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Practice ID | `SP_MARKET_COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE` |
| Component class | `PRIMARY_PRACTICE` |
| Status | Design only; not activated |

## Mission and Scope

Establish an evidence-based view of market definition, size, growth, demand, customers, competitors, ecosystems, access, and external uncertainty. Primary market analysis remains distinct from formal independent evidence validation.

## Core Questions

- Is the market real, accessible, sufficiently attractive, and correctly defined?
- Who competes, with what capabilities, economics, and likely response?
- What evidence supports customer demand, need, adoption, and willingness to pay?
- Which definitions, sources, or trends could reverse the conclusion?

## Required Inputs and Permitted Evidence

Required inputs include decision question, product/service boundary, customer/population, geography, period, channel, use case, source/tool authority, and commercial hypotheses. Permitted evidence includes official statistics, filings, primary research, authorized transaction/usage data, interviews, surveys, direct observations, competitor records, reputable transparent research, and qualified expert evidence.

## Analytical Methods

Bottom-up and top-down sizing with reconciliation, segmentation, competitive benchmarking, customer research, ecosystem mapping, demand-driver analysis, trend/scenario analysis, adoption analysis, sampling and uncertainty assessment, and source-lineage triangulation.

## Expected Outputs and Confidence

Outputs include market definition, sizing range, segment and competitor maps, demand-evidence report, customer findings, source-lineage map, assumptions, uncertainty, scenarios, and market inputs for downstream specialists. Material market conclusions require corroboration, formal independent validation, and Medium or High confidence for ordinary reliance.

## Materiality and Escalation

Material treatment applies when market or customer conclusions drive investment, entry, forecast, valuation, pricing, public policy, or external deliverables. Escalate opaque vendor methods, nonrepresentative samples, common-source lineage, conflicting definitions, stale evidence, privacy/consent concerns, insufficient demand evidence, or results highly sensitive to geography/population/period.

## Dependencies

Strategy defines the decision; Commercial tests monetization and channel execution; Finance consumes validated assumptions; Operations tests capacity; Technology tests product/data feasibility; Public Sector and Regulatory define institutional/jurisdiction constraints; Quantitative Science supports sampling, estimation, and experiments.

## Prohibited Actions and Non-Authorities

The practice cannot contact customers or competitors without authorization, represent estimates as facts, certify its own evidence, approve entry or pricing, authorize research incentives, or select sources to defend a preferred thesis. It does not own Finance forecasts or Commercial execution conclusions.

## Conflict and Correlation Risks

Vendor sponsorship, client-selected respondents, management framing, convenience samples, repeated reports from one dataset, consultant consensus, competitor hearsay, and survivorship/nonresponse bias. Multiple citations with common lineage count once.

## Required Audit Trail and Failure Conditions

Record definitions, population/geography/period, source provenance, access dates, samples/protocols, calculations, reconciliations, negative cases, contradictions, freshness, limitations, confidence, challenge, validation, and downstream consumers. Stop for no defensible market definition, inaccessible decision-critical evidence, unresolved Material source conflict, prohibited research, privacy uncertainty, or missing independent validation.

## Human Principal and Control Interfaces

Human Principal approval is required for external primary research, Sensitive-data use, Material reliance, and stage progression. `01_ORCHESTRATOR` controls routing, authorization, handoffs, assurance, and synthesis. Future Research/Evidence independently retrieves and validates sources; Independent Challenge tests alternative definitions and disconfirming demand; Risk/QA assesses evidence, privacy, and methodological risk; Deliverables preserves ranges and limitations.
