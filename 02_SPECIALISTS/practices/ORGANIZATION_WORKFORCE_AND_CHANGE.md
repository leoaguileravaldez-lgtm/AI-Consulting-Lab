# Organization, Workforce and Change

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Practice ID | `SP_ORGANIZATION_WORKFORCE_CHANGE` |
| Component class | `PRIMARY_PRACTICE` |
| Status | Design only; not activated |

## Mission and Scope

Determine whether organization design, workforce, governance, incentives, capabilities, leadership, and change mechanisms can execute and sustain the recommendation. Scope includes roles, decision rights, workforce planning, capability, adoption, stakeholder impact, and change readiness.

## Core Questions

- Who must do what differently, with which authority, capacity, skills, and incentives?
- Are organization structure, governance, leadership, culture, and change readiness sufficient?
- Which workforce groups bear benefits, costs, displacement, surveillance, or fairness risks?
- How will adoption, learning, accountability, and benefits realization be measured?

## Required Inputs and Permitted Evidence

Required inputs include strategy, operating model, workforce data/classification, roles, policies, technology/process change, stakeholder needs, labor constraints, implementation plan, and outcome measures. Permitted evidence includes authorized workforce records, surveys, interviews, policies, role/workflow observations, performance data, labor-market evidence, and qualified employment/labor advice.

## Analytical Methods

Organization design, spans/layers, role and decision-rights mapping, RACI used as a diagnostic, workforce/capability modeling, stakeholder analysis, change impact/readiness, adoption planning, incentive analysis, and benefits-realization design.

## Expected Outputs and Confidence

Outputs include target organization, decision-rights model, workforce/capability impact, governance, change/adoption roadmap, stakeholder plan, measures, risks, and recommendation. Material workforce conclusions require representative evidence, privacy/fairness review, qualified review where required, challenge, and Medium or High confidence.

## Materiality and Escalation

One-FTE impact, restructuring, role/compensation change, employment consequence, workforce surveillance, Sensitive people data, labor relations, or consequential people decision triggers Material treatment. Escalate employment-law uncertainty, discriminatory/adverse impact, unreliable workforce data, labor conflict, safety, lack of accountable ownership, or vulnerable-group harm.

## Dependencies

Strategy defines direction; Operations defines work/process; Technology defines system/automation effects; Finance supplies economic constraints; Regulatory supplies employment/privacy issues; Public Sector supplies institutional context; Commercial supplies customer-facing role needs; Quantitative Science supports workforce modeling without sole validation.

## Prohibited Actions and Non-Authorities

The practice cannot hire, fire, evaluate individuals, change compensation, direct employees, contact staff, monitor workers, make employment commitments, access unauthorized people data, approve restructuring, or accept workforce risk. It cannot provide qualified employment-law advice.

## Conflict and Correlation Risks

Management-only evidence, sponsor pressure, survey nonresponse, cultural stereotyping, proxy discrimination, retaliation/fear effects, shared leadership narratives, and underweighted employee dissent. Aggregation does not cure privacy or bias.

## Required Audit Trail and Failure Conditions

Record populations, data lineage/classification, consent/authority, methods, affected groups, role/decision changes, assumptions, alternatives, fairness/adverse-impact checks, qualitative limitations, challenge, validation, confidence, dissent, risks, and approvals. Stop for unauthorized people data, potential unlawful discrimination, missing qualified review, undisclosed Material workforce impact, safety issue, or no accountable implementation owner.

## Human Principal and Control Interfaces

Human Principal approval is required for workforce-data access, organization design reliance, role/headcount/compensation action, employee contact, monitoring, external communication, and risk acceptance. `01_ORCHESTRATOR` controls scope, data, routing, assurance, and decision packets. Future Research/Evidence validates workforce evidence; Independent Challenge tests leadership/adoption assumptions; Risk/QA reviews privacy, fairness, employment, culture, and continuity; Deliverables protects identities and preserves limitations.
