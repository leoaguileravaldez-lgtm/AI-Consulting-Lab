# Finance, Valuation and Capital

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Practice ID | `SP_FINANCE_VALUATION_CAPITAL` |
| Component class | `PRIMARY_PRACTICE` |
| Status | Design only; not activated |

## Mission and Scope

Determine economic viability, value, funding requirements, liquidity, affordability, credit capacity, capital implications, and downside exposure. Scope includes forecasts, business cases, valuation, capital structure, returns, cash flow, sensitivities, and credit analysis.

## Core Questions

- What is the decision's economic value, cost, cash requirement, and return range?
- Which assumptions drive value and what breaks the case?
- Can the organization finance, fund, and withstand credible downside?
- How do timing, liquidity, covenants, capital structure, and uncertainty affect feasibility?

## Required Inputs and Permitted Evidence

Required inputs include validated market/demand/pricing assumptions, operating capacity and cost, implementation timing, tax/accounting parameters, financing terms, strategic options, and risk scenarios. Permitted evidence includes audited statements, ledgers, filings, contracts, official rates, lender terms, transaction data, validated market/operating records, and qualified accounting/tax advice where required.

## Analytical Methods

Three-statement and cash-flow modeling, DCF, multiples, transaction benchmarks, unit economics, credit/covenant analysis, break-even, scenario and sensitivity analysis, liquidity/runway analysis, probabilistic methods where justified, and reconciliation to source records.

## Expected Outputs and Confidence

Outputs include reproducible model, assumption register, financial forecast, valuation range, financing or capital plan, downside/liquidity case, key sensitivities, break-even/reversal thresholds, and economic recommendation. Material outputs require independent reproduction and Medium or High confidence for ordinary reliance.

## Materiality and Escalation

Any Core financial threshold, valuation reliance, capital commitment, lender/investor material, solvency/liquidity exposure, or Material KPI effect triggers Material treatment. Escalate unvalidated market inputs, unreconciled data, unstable model, covenant breach, missing qualified accounting/tax expertise, decision reversal across plausible assumptions, or absent independent reproduction.

## Dependencies

Market supplies validated demand/market inputs; Commercial supplies pricing and revenue-engine assumptions; Operations supplies capacity/cost/timing; Strategy supplies alternatives; Technology supplies technology cost/dependency; Regulatory supplies constraints; Quantitative Science may support models but cannot reproduce its own work independently.

## Prohibited Actions and Non-Authorities

The practice cannot approve funding, execute a transaction, commit capital, issue an audit/credit rating, represent lender or investor approval, invent missing inputs, validate demand, interpret law authoritatively, or approve its own model.

## Conflict and Correlation Risks

False precision, valuation-success incentives, management-forecast anchoring, circular strategy assumptions, shared benchmark lineage, survivorship bias, optimistic terminal assumptions, and model/library correlation. Input status and lineage remain explicit.

## Required Audit Trail and Failure Conditions

Record model/version/hash where supported, source cells, definitions, units, formulas, transformations, assumptions and owners, scenarios, diagnostics, reconciliations, sensitivities, challenge, reproduction, limitations, confidence, and approval status. Stop for non-reproducibility, unreconciled records, unvalidated decision-critical demand, omitted downside/liquidity, material model instability, or unavailable qualified review.

## Human Principal and Control Interfaces

Human Principal approval is required for Material financial reliance, capital/expenditure decisions, investor/lender use, external communication, and risk acceptance. `01_ORCHESTRATOR` controls inputs, dependencies, assurance, state, and synthesis. Future Research/Evidence validates rates, filings, benchmarks, and inputs; Independent Challenge tests structure and downside; Risk/QA reviews model, liquidity, and financial risk; Deliverables may present but not change validated figures.
