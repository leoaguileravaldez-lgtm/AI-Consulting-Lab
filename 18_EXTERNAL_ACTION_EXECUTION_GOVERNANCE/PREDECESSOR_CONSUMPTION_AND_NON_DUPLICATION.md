# Predecessor Consumption and Non-Duplication

NORMATIVE_SOURCE: `CANONICAL_MODEL.json`  
NORMATIVE_SOURCE_VERSION: `1.0.0`  
DERIVATION_TYPE: predecessor and authority-boundary projection

Layer 18 consumes all eighteen exact predecessor contracts in `predecessor_contracts`. Every reference requires exact owner, object identity, version/hash, scope, purpose, client/entity/engagement, target, jurisdiction, confidentiality, lifecycle, freshness, expiry, revocation, limitations and validation state where applicable. Missing never means inapplicable.

| Certified owner | Retained responsibility | Layer 18 consumption only |
|---|---|---|
| 00–06 | Core/Human governance, orchestration, specialist, engagement, evidence, challenge, QA and risk | Exact action authority, boundaries, evidence, qualifications, controls and limitations; no reinterpretation or self-approval |
| 07–10 | Artifact/release/delivery, knowledge/reuse, client operations and client isolation | Exact artifact, recipient/channel, provenance and client boundary references; no release-to-dispatch or cross-boundary inference |
| 11–13 | Workflow, concurrency/retry, decision/compliance/SOD, identity/access/authorization/revocation | Exact work, decision, approval, actor/executor and currentness references; no workflow/decision/access-to-action escalation |
| 14 | Sole protected-action runtime enforcement, global effect slot and E23 barrier | Exact current action/domain/attempt/slot/permit/mediation/consumption/target/revalidation/barrier binding; no second enforcement model or barrier creation |
| 15 | Engagement/evidence/conclusion/decision/delivery-package and non-executing handoff | Exact applicable scope only; no handoff-as-instruction or outcome-trace authority |
| 16 | Operational-realization conformance | Exact applicable integrity, uniqueness, fencing, recovery, isolation, telemetry and empirical evidence obligations; no implementation claim |
| 17 | Independent deployment assurance and non-authorizing admission | Exact current assurance/admission reference only when applicable to execution infrastructure; no authorization, observation or effect inference |

Layer 18 cannot fill, validate, mutate, supersede or broaden a predecessor record. A stronger execution mechanism cannot cure invalid authority. Dependency direction is forward only:

`certified predecessor authority and semantics → Layer 18 bounded attempt and execution evidence → future independent reconciliation authority`.

The future reconciler may consume Layer 18 evidence but may not point backward to manufacture authorization or retroactively validate an invalid dispatch.
