# Canonical Orchestrator State Machine

## Authority and Rule

This is the sole canonical lifecycle specification. Every permitted transition is listed below. Any source-target pair not listed is prohibited. A transition commits only when its actor, preconditions, evidence, approval, audit event, expected state/version, fencing token, and idempotency requirements pass.

## Controlled State Vocabulary

- **Active:** `DRAFT_INTAKE`, `CONFLICT_CHECK`, `ENGAGEMENT_AUTHORIZATION`, `CLASSIFY`, `PLAN`, `PLAN_REVIEW`, `DELEGATE`, `ANALYZE`, `CHALLENGE`, `REMEDIATE`, `VALIDATE_EVIDENCE`, `VALIDATE_ANALYSIS`, `RISK_REVIEW`, `SYNTHESIZE`, `SYNTHESIS_INTEGRITY_REVIEW`, `RELEASE_CHECK`, `HUMAN_REVIEW`, `DELIVERY_AUTHORIZATION`, `DELIVER`, `DELIVERY_VERIFICATION`, `RETAIN`, `ARCHIVE`, `CLOSE_REVIEW`.
- **Blocked/failed/hold:** `BLOCKED_EVIDENCE`, `BLOCKED_CONFIDENCE`, `BLOCKED_SOURCE_CONFLICT`, `BLOCKED_VALIDATION`, `BLOCKED_AUTHORIZATION`, `BLOCKED_PERMISSION`, `BLOCKED_CONFLICT`, `BLOCKED_POLICY_CONFLICT`, `BLOCKED_DELEGATION`, `BLOCKED_CAPACITY`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `SYNTHESIS_FAILED`, `SECURITY_HOLD`, `INCIDENT_HOLD`, `EXPIRED_APPROVAL`, `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`.
- **Terminal:** `CLOSED`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`, `SUPERSEDED`.

## Universal Guard and Failure Rule

Every transition requires the expected source state/version, current lease/fencing token, unused or matching idempotency key, immutable engagement match, eligible actor/role, permission tier, scope/data authority, current classification/risk, required record cardinality and referential integrity, security clearance, and successful atomic commit defined in `RECORD_SCHEMAS_AND_AUDIT.md`.

The `Failure behavior` column identifies the applicable fail-closed outcome but does not itself mutate state. A failed forward transition leaves its source state/version unchanged until one explicit F-series entry transition below atomically commits the blocked/hold/failed state, opens its canonical block/hold record, appends its audit event, and applies the narrowest breaker. If even that audit/state commit is unavailable, the source state remains authoritative and the independently protected health breaker denies further transitions until reconciliation. There is no implicit or generic failure transition.

## Canonical Transition-Actor Rules

The `Authorized actor` column uses only the canonical control-role vocabulary in `RECORD_SCHEMAS_AND_AUDIT.md`. A named role initiates or attests the transition; the trusted `WORKFLOW_CONTROLLER` commits the state change through the atomic transition protocol. A state mutation is a Tier 3 reversible internal control-plane action unless N23 or N24 makes it a Tier 4 external/material action. Therefore every transition requires the prior task-specific or standing human authorization required for Tier 3, and N23/N24 require explicit human authorization tied to the exact Tier 4 action, under `00_CORE/approval_policy/APPROVAL_POLICY.md`. The matrix column “Human Principal approval required” identifies when the Human Principal personally, rather than a valid scoped human delegate, must approve; “No” never means that the underlying Tier 3 human authorization may be omitted. Analytical work preceding a transition remains within its separately authorized Tier 0–2 scope. Role eligibility requires an active, engagement-scoped control-role assignment and attributable actor/session identity in both the transition journal and audit event.

“Delegation permitted” below means assignment only to another actor already authorized for the same canonical role, engagement, scope, tier, expiry, and separation-of-duties constraints. It never permits role invention, authority transfer, tier elevation, or approval delegation beyond `00_CORE`. “Conditional” non-delegability means the approval model, engagement terms, risk limit, or `00_CORE` may reserve the decision to the Human Principal. Exact-object approval binds the stated artifact, action, version/hash, scope, conditions, and validity period; it cannot be reused for a changed object.

## Canonical Blocked/Hold/Failed Entry Transitions

The entries below are the only permitted transitions into a blocked, failed, hold, expired-approval, or recovery-required state. The named source sets are exact controlled sets, not implementation wildcards:

- `ACTIVE_ALL` means every state in the Active vocabulary above.
- `ACTIVE_THROUGH_HUMAN_REVIEW` means `DRAFT_INTAKE`, `CONFLICT_CHECK`, `ENGAGEMENT_AUTHORIZATION`, `CLASSIFY`, `PLAN`, `PLAN_REVIEW`, `DELEGATE`, `ANALYZE`, `CHALLENGE`, `REMEDIATE`, `VALIDATE_EVIDENCE`, `VALIDATE_ANALYSIS`, `RISK_REVIEW`, `SYNTHESIZE`, `SYNTHESIS_INTEGRITY_REVIEW`, `RELEASE_CHECK`, and `HUMAN_REVIEW`.
- `RECOVERABLE_NONTERMINAL` means `ACTIVE_ALL` plus `BLOCKED_EVIDENCE`, `BLOCKED_CONFIDENCE`, `BLOCKED_SOURCE_CONFLICT`, `BLOCKED_VALIDATION`, `BLOCKED_AUTHORIZATION`, `BLOCKED_PERMISSION`, `BLOCKED_CONFLICT`, `BLOCKED_POLICY_CONFLICT`, `BLOCKED_DELEGATION`, `BLOCKED_CAPACITY`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `SYNTHESIS_FAILED`, and `EXPIRED_APPROVAL`. It excludes `SECURITY_HOLD`, `INCIDENT_HOLD`, and `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`.
- `ALL_NONTERMINAL_EXCEPT_INCIDENT` means `RECOVERABLE_NONTERMINAL` plus `SECURITY_HOLD` and `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`; only `INCIDENT_HOLD` is excluded.

Each entry is a Tier 3 reversible internal containment transition under prior task-specific or standing human control-plane authorization. “None additional” below means no new case-specific approval is required to fail closed; it does not waive that Tier 3 authorization. The canonical actor must have an active engagement-scoped role assignment and cannot use a failure entry to obtain new access, authority, approval, or recovery rights.

| F14 | `ALL_NONTERMINAL_EXCEPT_INCIDENT` | `INCIDENT_HOLD` | Critical security event, audit/identity/permission/approval/control-plane corruption, confirmed cross-client access, unreconciled material control failure, or failed recovery whose corruption/outcome cannot be established | `INCIDENT_AUTHORITY`, `SECURITY_REVIEWER`, `AUDIT_VERIFIER`, or `RECOVERY_COORDINATOR` | Tier 3 containment | Incident threshold met or trusted control/recovery integrity cannot be established | Incident or failed-reconciliation record, preserved evidence, affected engagement/object inventory and breaker scope | None additional | Atomically open type `INCIDENT`; breaker mandatory; permitted target F18 | `INCIDENT_HOLD_OPENED` | F18, then R02/R03 as applicable |
| F15 | `HUMAN_REVIEW`, `DELIVERY_AUTHORIZATION`, or `DELIVER` | `EXPIRED_APPROVAL` | Exact approval expires after prior validation/reservation but before governed use or completion; no external effect may proceed | `APPROVAL_WRITER` or `WORKFLOW_CONTROLLER` | Tier 3 containment | Expiration verified; action outcome known to be not dispatched, otherwise F16/F14 applies | Approval/version/expiry, reservation status, artifact/action comparison and dispatch evidence | None additional | Atomically open type `EXPIRED_APPROVAL`; permitted targets B09/B10 | `APPROVAL_EXPIRY_HOLD_OPENED` | B09 or B10 |
| F16 | `RECOVERABLE_NONTERMINAL` | `RECOVERY_REQUIRED` | Stale state/version, lease/fence conflict, duplicate or concurrent write, crash/partial commit, runtime/control failure, corrupted checkpoint, ambiguous journal, approval reconciliation, or unknown partial action requires controlled recovery | `HEALTH_CONTROLLER`, `RECOVERY_INITIATOR`, `AUDIT_VERIFIER`, or `WORKFLOW_CONTROLLER` | Tier 3 containment | Normal forward processing is unsafe; no confirmed incident requiring F14 instead | Detection record, expected/observed versions, lease/fence, journals, approval/action status, affected dependencies and breaker scope | None additional | Atomically open type `RECOVERY`; breaker mandatory; permitted targets R02/R03; failed reconciliation enters F14 | `RECOVERY_REQUIRED_OPENED` | R02, R03, or F14 |
| F17 | `SECURITY_HOLD` | `RECOVERY_REQUIRED` | Security containment and boundary verification permit controlled recovery preparation | `RECOVERY_AUTHORIZER` + `SECURITY_REVIEWER` | Tier 3 recovery entry | Containment verified; affected scope and trusted control path established; hold cannot be reset directly | Security/incident verification, impact scope, breaker status, recovery plan and independent verification | Recovery authorization; Human Principal where `00_CORE` requires | Atomically resolve the type `SECURITY` record and open a new type `RECOVERY` record linked to it; the breaker remains open | `SECURITY_HOLD_RELEASED_TO_RECOVERY` | R02/R03 or F14 |
| F18 | `INCIDENT_HOLD` | `RECOVERY_REQUIRED` | Incident containment, evidence preservation, and authorized resumption permit controlled recovery preparation | `INCIDENT_AUTHORITY` + `RECOVERY_AUTHORIZER` | Tier 3 recovery entry | Incident disposition complete enough for recovery; trusted authority path re-established; hold cannot be reset directly | Incident disposition, affected-state inventory, preserved evidence, breaker status, recovery plan and independent verification | Human Principal resumption approval for Material/Critical and any non-delegable matter; recovery authorization | Atomically resolve the type `INCIDENT` record and open a new type `RECOVERY` record linked to it; the breaker remains open | `INCIDENT_HOLD_RELEASED_TO_RECOVERY` | R02/R03 or renewed F14 |

Every F-series transition requires expected source state/version, current fence or independently protected containment authority, immutable engagement match, an atomically opened canonical block/hold record, and its specified audit event. If audit/control-plane integrity prevents atomic entry, the independently protected breaker denies advancement and F14 is required once the incident record path is available. Any transition into a blocked, failed, hold, expired-approval, or recovery-required state not explicitly listed above is prohibited.

## Terminal-State Rule

`CLOSED`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`, and `SUPERSEDED` have no outbound transitions. Correction or renewed work creates a new task ID with explicit lineage to the terminal task. Terminal history is never reopened or overwritten.
