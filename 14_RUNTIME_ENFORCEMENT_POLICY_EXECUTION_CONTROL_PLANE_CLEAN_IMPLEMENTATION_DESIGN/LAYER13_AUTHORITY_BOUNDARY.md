E01 requires those exact source IDs, versions, hashes and current states. It may carry only the certified bounded authorization into Layer 14. E02–E04 introduce those Layer 14 contracts through separately authorized transformations. The mandate issuer must be authorized by E01, must satisfy SOD, and cannot be the subject, event acceptor, or closure authority where separation is required. Required Human Principal authority remains final and non-proxyable.
