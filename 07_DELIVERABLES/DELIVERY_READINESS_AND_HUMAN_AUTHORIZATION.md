# Delivery Readiness and Human Authorization

## Advisory artifact results

Layer 07 may record `DELIVERABLE_PREPARED`, `DELIVERABLE_REVIEWED`, `DELIVERABLE_INTERNALLY_CONSISTENT`, `NOT_PRESENTATION_READY`, `SEMANTIC_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `SECURITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `REVALIDATION_REQUIRED`.

These are artifact metadata only. They are not workflow states, synthesis-integrity results, release results, approval, blocker resolution, risk acceptance, delivery authorization or engagement status.

`DELIVERABLE_PREPARED ≠ DELIVERABLE_REVIEWED ≠ DELIVERABLE_INTERNALLY_CONSISTENT ≠ AUTHORIZED_FOR_EXTERNAL_DELIVERY`.

## Human Principal wording requests

Requests to make wording stronger, softer, shorter, more persuasive or more executive require semantic comparison. If Material meaning, claim strength, confidence, uncertainty, risk, dissent, causality, limitation or professional status changes, the revision is a content change and returns upstream. Human authority cannot rewrite analytical truth.

## External-action prohibition

`READY`, `FINAL`, `GENERATED`, `RENDERED`, `SIGNED`, `APPROVED_FOR_REVIEW` and similar labels grant no action authority. Layer 07 cannot SEND, EMAIL, UPLOAD, FILE, SUBMIT, PUBLISH, SHARE, TRANSMIT, DELIVER, MESSAGE, POST, CONNECT, consume approval or select a delivery channel.

Only the certified exact-artifact Human approval, `DELIVERY_AUTHORIZATION`, fenced executor and Tier 4 transaction may permit external action.
