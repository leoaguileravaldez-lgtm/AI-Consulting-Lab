# Audience Adaptation and Disclosure

## Audience profiles

Authorized profiles may support Board, CEO, CFO, lender, investor, government, public, technical, operational and client-executive audiences. Adaptation may change structure, detail, terminology and format but not governed meaning.

Executive compression preserves the decision, alternatives, Material assumptions, limitations, uncertainty, dissent, residual risk, reversal conditions, professional-review requirements and dependencies needed for correct interpretation. Technical expansion may only expose governed detail; it cannot invent analysis.

## Disclosure treatment

Each Material disclosure is visibly presented, incorporated by reference, placed in an authorized appendix, or omitted only under a documented non-Material determination. If confidentiality prevents disclosure, the advisory treatment is one of:

- `RESTRICTED_APPENDIX_REQUIRED`;
- `ALTERNATE_AUDIENCE_VERSION_REQUIRED`;
- `DISCLOSURE_WITHHELD_BY_POLICY`;
- `AUDIENCE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`;
- `DECISION_CONTEXT_INCOMPLETE`;
- `NON_MISLEADING_QUALIFICATION_REQUIRED`;
- `NO_SAFE_DELIVERABLE_AVAILABLE`.

These are disclosure findings, not states or access decisions. Redaction may not silently erase meaning. When no truthful, non-misleading version can be produced, the artifact fails readiness.

Public-safe and confidential variants remain exact-version artifacts with separate disclosure and hidden-content reviews.
