# Multiformat Consistency

PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, HTML, executive summaries, board memoranda, bank packages, technical annexes and email drafts belonging to one artifact family bind to one exact canonical deliverable and content version.

The family manifest compares every Material recommendation, claim, number, unit, currency, period, scenario, assumption, confidence, uncertainty, dissent, residual risk, professional-review status, Human Principal disposition, reversal condition and dependency.

Comparison results are `EQUIVALENT`, `EQUIVALENT_WITH_NON_MATERIAL_PRESENTATION_DIFFERENCES`, `MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE` or `UNKNOWN`. These are advisory artifact results only. `MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE` and `UNKNOWN` prevent clean readiness.

Text equality alone is insufficient. Comparison includes omission, ordering, titles, captions, footnotes, disclosure placement, chart framing, visual prominence, redaction and hidden content. Stale chart caches, copied recommendations, differing appendices and inconsistent Human dispositions are failures.

Each format records renderer/method version and inspection limitations. Future tooling may assist, but Material equivalence requires the independence and review prescribed by certified controls.
