## Residual limitations

Architecture cannot perfectly detect subtle rhetorical distortion or covert collusion. Visual salience requires human judgment. Physical segregation, format-specific hidden-content parsers, controlled rendering environments, identity/access enforcement and deterministic semantic-diff tooling remain future governed implementation. Large artifact families may impose significant review cost.
