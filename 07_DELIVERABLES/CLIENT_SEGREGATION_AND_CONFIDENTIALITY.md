# Client Segregation and Confidentiality

Every artifact, template instance, statement, citation, chart, table, note, comment, property, cache, embedded object, attachment, lineage relation and review record carries the immutable engagement ID and authorized client/security domain.

Client-specific facts, financial data, charts, recommendations, appendices, metadata, model outputs, hidden content and caches cannot cross engagements. Similarity does not authorize reuse. Reusable methodology or templates may cross boundaries only when authorized and verified free of client identity or client-derived content.

Future verification must inspect visible text plus metadata, tracked changes, comments, notes, speaker notes, hidden slides, hidden rows/columns/sheets, hidden text, embedded objects, attachments, chart source data, alternate text, stale caches, external links, headers/footers, filenames and document properties.

Unsupported formats or incomplete inspection produce `UNKNOWN` and fail closed. Cross-client identity or content causes denial, quarantine, audit notification and canonical security escalation.

`INTERNAL`, `CLIENT_CONFIDENTIAL`, `EXECUTIVE_RESTRICTED`, `PUBLIC_SAFE`, `REGULATED_RESTRICTED` and `DRAFT` are intended-distribution metadata mapped to certified security rules. They do not authenticate, grant access, select recipients or authorize transmission.
