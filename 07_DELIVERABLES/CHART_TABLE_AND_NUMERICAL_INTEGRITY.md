# Chart, Table, and Numerical Integrity

## Numerical authority

Layer 07 renders governed quantitative results. It does not own valuation, forecasting, financial or engineering modeling, statistical inference, optimization, simulation, scenario computation, causal estimation or quantitative certification.

Each Material displayed value references its exact source model/output version, field/cell/series, unit, currency and conversion source, nominal/real basis, period/as-of date, scenario, denominator/population, transformation and rounding rule.

Display transformations must be deterministic, traceable, unit-safe, reproducible and non-semantic. A transformation capable of changing a decision returns to authorized analytical or quantitative capability.

## Deterministic checks

Checks reconcile summaries, bodies, charts, tables, appendices and format variants; totals/subtotals; units; currency; periods; scenarios; denominators; nominal/real basis; percent/percentage points; source versions; and Material rounding impact.

## Visual integrity

Charts and tables must prohibit deceptive axis truncation, cherry-picked periods, omitted Material series, hidden denominators, misleading aggregation, unsupported causal titles, stale caches, inconsistent labels, scenario/fact confusion, and visual minimization of risk or dissent. Axis breaks and exclusions require visible explanation. Beauty and branding never outweigh semantic fidelity.
