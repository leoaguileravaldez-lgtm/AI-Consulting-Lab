# Citation and Source Rendering

`04_RESEARCH_EVIDENCE` remains the exclusive authority for source identity, evidence status, provenance, lineage, quality, freshness, applicability, corroboration and contradiction. Layer 07 renders only exact authorized citation records.

Every rendered Material citation binds the source/evidence version, supported claim portion, root lineage, jurisdiction, freshness/as-of state, applicability, authorized display variant and limitations.

Citation profiles may support internal traceability, client-facing, regulatory/technical and public-safe display. Style and visible density may vary without changing identity or support.

Layer 07 cannot invent sources, fabricate citations, substitute a source, broaden a proposition, change quality or applicability, hide client-provided status, change confidence, declare independence, or convert citation volume into corroboration.

Wrong-source attachment, missing support, inaccessible or stale version, fabricated identifier, citation laundering, lineage mismatch and confidential metadata leakage fail citation review. Internal lineage remains complete even when client-facing citations are compressed.
