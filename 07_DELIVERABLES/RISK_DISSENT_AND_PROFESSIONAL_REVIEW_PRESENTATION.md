# Risk, Dissent, and Professional-Review Presentation

## Risk

Layer 07 presents exact canonical risk classification, residual risk, mitigation status, open issue and QA references. It cannot recompute, reclassify, accept, average away or visually minimize risk. Critical and Material risk receive prominence proportionate to decision relevance.

## Dissent

Material decision packets preserve the original recommendation, challenge, response, revision, unresolved dissent, minority view where applicable, residual uncertainty, reversal conditions and Human Principal disposition. Resolved dissent includes its exact resolution reference. Majority vote, confidence averaging, narrative compression or visual hierarchy cannot create consensus.

Restricted/public artifacts need not expose raw internal debate, but they cannot falsely imply consensus. If a truthful abstraction is impossible, the version fails readiness.

## Professional review

Layer 07 may accurately present whether review is required or completed, the exact review reference, governed identity/qualification metadata, scope/version reviewed, documented outcome, limitations and conditions. It cannot infer, fabricate, broaden, upgrade, reinterpret or replace the professional conclusion or imply certification where none exists.

Human Principal disposition remains separate from analytical validation, challenge resolution, QA status, professional opinion and risk acceptance.
