# Deliverables Architecture

## Purpose and subordination

`07_DELIVERABLES` constructs governed presentation artifacts from exact upstream objects without changing semantic meaning. It operationalizes presentation support for the canonical deliverable record, synthesis-integrity gate, release controls, approvals, audit, and state owned by `01_ORCHESTRATOR`. If this package conflicts with a certified rule, the certified rule controls and affected work fails closed.

`07` is not an analytical synthesizer, recommendation authority, evidence authority, challenger, QA or risk authority, professional reviewer, approver, release controller, workflow controller, executor, connector, or Human Principal proxy.

## Semantic invariants

- Presentation may increase clarity.
- Presentation may not increase claim strength.
- Presentation may not reduce Material uncertainty.
- Presentation may not convert dissent into consensus.
- Presentation may not reduce Material risk through visual or narrative design.
- Presentation may not convert assumptions into facts.
- Presentation may not convert scenarios into observed outcomes.
- Presentation may not convert client-provided information into independently verified external data.
- Unknown semantic equivalence fails closed.

## Ownership boundary

`00_CORE` owns governance, security, materiality and approvals. `01` owns the canonical deliverable, synthesis review, workflow, release and external-action path. `02` owns analysis and calculations. `03` owns engagement context. `04` owns evidence and citation identity. `05` owns challenge and dissent. `06` owns process QA and residual-risk assessment. `07` owns presentation artifacts, lineage projections, display specifications and advisory fidelity checks only.

Material synthesis or new substantive meaning remains upstream and must undergo the applicable canonical analysis, evidence, challenge, QA, synthesis-integrity and Human review processes.

## Non-authority

`07` cannot create or modify analysis, evidence, challenge, dissent, QA, risk, professional conclusions, Human decisions, approvals, blockers, canonical readiness, state or audit authority. It cannot accept risk, waive review, authorize release, consume approval, or perform external action. Preparation, rendering, signing and final appearance are not authorization.
