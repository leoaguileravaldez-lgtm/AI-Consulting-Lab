# Versioning, Supersession, and Invalidation

## Content and presentation versions

`CONTENT_VERSION` changes whenever governed semantic content changes, including wording, claims, recommendations, numbers, definitions, assumptions, scenarios, confidence, uncertainty, limitations, dissent, risk, professional status, Human disposition or Material inclusion/omission.

`PRESENTATION_VERSION` changes for each artifact rendering. Presentation-only treatment is limited to proven equivalent typography, spacing, pagination, accessibility tagging, non-semantic layout and equivalent format conversion. Every artifact has a new hash.

Prior approval never silently transfers. A formatting change avoids full analytical revalidation only when an independent impact check proves unchanged content, claims, disclosures, risk, dissent, professional status, audience/purpose and visual-semantic meaning. Otherwise `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

## Artifact metadata

`ACTIVE`, `SUPERSEDED`, `STALE`, `REVALIDATION_REQUIRED` and `WITHDRAWN` are deliverable-record metadata only, never canonical task or engagement states. Old versions remain immutable and auditable.

## Invalidation

Material change to scope, content, evidence, model, assumption, challenge, dissent, QA, risk, professional review, jurisdiction, Human decision, approval, recipient, channel, confidentiality or intended use deterministically affects dependent artifacts. `07` reflects `STALE` or `REVALIDATION_REQUIRED` and sends an advisory impact notice to `01`.

`07` cannot invalidate analysis, revoke approval, open or resolve blockers, reopen tasks, alter readiness or change canonical state.
